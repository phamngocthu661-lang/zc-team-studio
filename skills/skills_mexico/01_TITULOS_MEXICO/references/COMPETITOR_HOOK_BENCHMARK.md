## Benchmark axes — use these verbatim
1. OFFENSE VISIBILITY: what visible violation makes a Mexican audience angry in <=3 seconds?
2. RELATIONAL CONTRAST: who is chosen instead of mother/grandmother, who exploits whose house/effort?
3. MATERIAL / EMOTIONAL STAKES: which family bond, space, asset, tradition is immediately threatened?
4. ACTIVE REVERSAL SIGNAL: does lead decisively refuse, show rights, withdraw support, expose contradiction, face a family member? Simple evidence discovery is weaker.
5. CONSEQUENTIAL OPEN LOOP: after the act, what concrete choice/humiliation/confrontation remains unseen?

### Mandatory adversarial examples (not copy-ready production titles)
- `Mi hijo vendió mis máquinas de coser sin permiso; encontré el anuncio que lo delató`: credible but evidence-only ending → capped reversal 8, cannot win gate.
- `Mis hijos repartieron mi casa en el chat familiar; les hice una sola pregunta`: visible offense, weak counteraction unless question triggers a decisive collision.
- `Mi yerno se burló de mi puesto del mercado… luego me suplicó que fuera su aval`: insult–dependence contrast creates strong potential, **only usable if aval's role/financial risk are credible**.
