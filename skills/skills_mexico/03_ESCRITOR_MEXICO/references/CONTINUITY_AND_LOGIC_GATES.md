# MEXICAN FAMILY LOGIC GATES FOR WRITER

1. **Relationships:** full map of mother, adult son/daughter, spouse, consuegra, suegra and grandparents. Never call the protagonist's son's mother-in-law `mi suegra`; use `mi consuegra` if narrator is the adult son's mother.
2. **Time:** day/month/year, retirement dates, wedding dates, deceased spouse, children ages, messages, meeting, notary/bank timeline and aftermath. No one travels cross-country instantly.
3. **Money:** amounts in pesos (MXN) when used, earnings and bank balance, who controls accounts, the owner of each transfer. Grandma cannot cancel someone's personal card by phoning a bank without authorization.
4. **Home:** deed versus utility bill; ownership versus possession/tenant status; notice and due process plausible. "Las escrituras están a mi nombre" is not itself proof if contested; evidence handling must be credible.
5. **Evidence:** screen captures can be forged or out of context; show authentication/corroboration for consequential accusations. A neighbor cannot know a locked safe's contents without a credible opportunity.
6. **Character POV:** narrator knows her own observations and disclosed facts, not every private meeting. Sequences reconstructed later must identify source.
7. **Dialogue:** consistent usted/tú; older adults aren't all saintly; in-law's behavior has motivation and plausible social pressure, not dramatic villain recitation.
8. **Authority:** Mexican state-specific inheritance, eviction, marital regime and evidence laws vary; avoid categorical procedural claims, consult/verify if story depends on precise outcome. No US jury verdict or US county clerk transplanted into Mexican town.
9. **Children:** grandchildren not used as hostages for titillation. If threats to restrict visits appear, the emotional/ethical cost is present and adults respond responsibly.
10. **Social consequences:** neighbors, wedding guests, families don't instantly all side with narrator just because of one sentence. Social proof comes from believable sequence.
11. **Trauma / violence:** no poisoning how-to, self-harm glamor or abuse punish fantasies; if a dangerous act occurs handle consequence seriously and avoid detailed mechanisms.
12. **Ending:** earlier evidence props are resolved; separate financial/legal closure from relationship closure; no unexplained total reconciliation or magical emotional cure.

Per-Part checkpoint: `KNOWN_FACTS | PROTAGONIST_NEW_KNOWLEDGE | ANTAGONIST_NEW_KNOWLEDGE | OWNERSHIP_STATE | MONEY_STATE | DOCUMENT_CUSTODY | ACTIVE_LOOPS | CHOICE/COST | STRONG_SPANS`. Keep outside manuscript as internal QA notes.
