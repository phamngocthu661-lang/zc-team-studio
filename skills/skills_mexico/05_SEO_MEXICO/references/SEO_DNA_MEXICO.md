# SEO KEYWORDS & PACKAGING DNA FOR MEXICO

## Relevance-first cluster
Core: `historias de familia, historias de vida, drama familiar, traición familiar, relato emotivo, madre, abuela, hijo, hija, nuera, consuegra, dignidad, respeto familiar, límites familiares`.
Specific depending on actual script: `cumpleaños, boda, Navidad, cena familiar, escrituras, casa, recámara, notaría, herencia, testamento, cuenta bancaria, ahorro, nietos, mensaje, desaire, humillación`.
Use semantic phrases viewers could plausibly search; avoid unrelated channels, repetitive misspelling storms, and automatic keyword dumps.

## Description structure (90–160 words default)
1. First sentence reenacts the exact emotional title event in original words.
2. One-sentence protagonist circumstances and price of being treated as invisible.
3. Source-grounded clue that starts changing her decision without final reveal.
4. One reason to keep listening, a unique philosophical question embedded in action.
5. Optional gentle invitation to reflect or subscribe; no generic artificial "este video cambiará tu vida" claim.
No hashtags in body, no emojis/icons unless requested, no invented legal wins, no fabricated city/names. Never tag fiction "historia real" unless evidence supplied.

## Alternative titles
Secondary A: action/decision angle, B: symbolic object/emotional reversal angle. Each ≤100 Unicode characters. Never silently replace locked primary. Only alternatives, not A/B test results; measured CTR requires actual YouTube experiment.
