## 1. Market, form and niche
- MARKET: viewers listening in Mexico. Script language: **natural Mexican Spanish (es-MX)**, neutral enough across Mexican states; no generic English or European Spanish. Coordination, audit and production metadata in Vietnamese by default.
- Niche: **madres / abuelas adultas × traición familiar × despojo o humillación × dignidad y límites × reversión justificada × cierre emocional**. Not mafia, homelessness, property renovation or US court drama.
- Narration: first-person adult/older female by default, ages 58–78, with a concrete former/present vocation, vulnerabilities, competence and emotional ambiguity. Other viewpoints possible ONLY if requested and clearly locked at Step2.
- Protagonist must actively change the situation through human choices and verifiable evidence, not a miraculous hidden billionaire identity. Antagonists need recognizable incentives; no cartoon malice, no dangerous revenge tutorials.
- Family boundaries and proportionate consequences, not sadistic humiliation, are the lasting emotional reward.
- Never claim **historia real**, **caso verdadero**, real names or legal outcomes without user-provided factual substantiation. Fiction can be sold as relato/historia narrativa without deceptive documentary framing.

## 4. Early-retention contract for Mexican family drama
Within initial script body, not counting headings:
1. ≤200 codepoints: directly touch the exact title event with one concrete affront, message, quote or denial.
2. ≤450: show proof the protagonist can observe (invitation, message, keys, ownership note, seating, canceled transfer) not vague foreshadowing.
3. ≤750: demonstrate the direct relational or material stakes.
4. ≤1,300: deliver an actionable micro-payoff or protagonist's precise next action, no extended biography.
5. ≤2,300: establish consequential obstacle / second question.
6. ≤5,000: deliver second evidence beat and an irreversible minor decision.
NO long channel introduction, CTA or chronology-reset monologue before these. Avoid prematurely giving away the final resolution. Throughout: a meaningful progress / reversal / revaluation roughly every 900–1,800 chars; vary pacing instead of a literal timer. Hold at least three open loops total, never invent false mysteries.

## 5. Seven-Part causal contract
Part1: public/private injustice + first reaction + observable clue.
Part2: family pattern, concrete stakes, protagonist's informed choice.
Part3: first boundary, credible resistance, partial evidence, cost.
Part4: midpoint: apparent reversal, new price to pay, protagonist makes a tougher choice.
Part5: antagonist's escalation or buried relationship truth, evidence chain verified.
Part6: public or private confrontation grounded in documents/witnesses/actions, consequence with due process.
Part7: human aftershock, differentiated accountability, earned repair/limited contact/no contact, ending-by-action rather than lecture.
Do not force a courtroom, surprise inheritance, huge bank account, or a complete happy reconciliation if title/evidence does not warrant it.

## 6. Non-negotiable logic gates
- Same names, ages, kinship terms, location, chronology, rooms, keys, dates, amounts and source of knowledge across 7 parts.
- Property control: escritura/título, titularidad, copropiedad, contrato, arrendamiento, powers, and rights must be set in canon and checked with local counsel if specific law is asserted. **Paying predial or a mortgage does not by itself establish ownership**; don't simplify legal jurisdiction without checking.
- Bank/account: distinguish account holder, authorized access and transaction limits. No magical account takeover; no instantaneous freezes or eviction without authority.
- Proof: no conveniently omniscient overheard dialogue; show how messages/docs/recordings were lawfully obtained. Consent/privacy constraints where pertinent.
- Speech: do not confuse **nuera (son's wife)**, **yerno (daughter's husband)**, **consuegra (child's spouse's mother)**, **suegra (mother-in-law)**; names and pronouns must remain stable.
- Locality: Mexican words/setting authentic in small, plot-functional details (escrituras, colonia, notaría, predial, mercado, recámara, recibo, quincena), no stereotypes. Avoid Spanish from Spain (*vosotros*, *vale*, *piso* as apartment) and non-Mexico US jurisdiction (*condado*, US jury, "county clerk") unless justified by setting.
- Emotional ethics: independent agency, realistic grief, dignity; avoid life-threatening revenge, poison, abuse fetishization. If violence occurs, handle briefly, responsibly and with credible safety consequences.
- YouTube: no defamatory claims about real individuals; avoid sensational claims unsupported by script; respectful advertiser-suitable tone.
