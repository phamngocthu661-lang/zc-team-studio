## Emotional promise to fulfill
In the examples supplied, the core is not simply "revenge". It is the cost to a mother/grandmother who gave years of unpaid emotional or practical labor and is now treated as expendable. The story must prove what she sacrificed without making her infallible, give her a credible role in uncovering truth and protecting her rights, and reveal the internal price of finally saying NO. Confrontation can be private; public humiliation and court spectacle are optional, never mandatory.

## Execution pipeline
1. **TITLE PROMISE DECODING**: quote exact key words from locked title, identify betrayal, who knows it, what narrator thinks, open question and minimally satisfying resolution.
2. **PRIMARY STORY DNA**: choose one of six in `STORY_DNA.md`, optional one secondary. Write why another DNA would create an incoherent story.
3. **CHARACTER ENGINE**: lead is 58–78 by default; establish profession/savings source, health and limitations, beliefs/fear, access, family history, contradiction, changing desire and agency. Son/daughter/nuera/consuegra each own motive, risk, conflicting needs and a potential response to boundaries. Do NOT have every younger person be evil.
4. **REASONABLE SETTING**: choose one Mexican place (state, colonia, city), housing type, living arrangement, travel time and income context; ground by mundane story-functional details without invented local jurisdiction rules.
5. **BATTLEFIELD**: define one concrete threatened resource (home, bedroom, business, bank support, access to grandchildren, reputation, inheritance) and who can LEGALLY or PRACTICALLY do what. State exact legal unknowns explicitly for verification rather than claiming immediate reversals.
6. **REVEAL ENGINE**: at least 6 causally useful evidentiary beats and 4 emotional reinterpretations, each set up before payoff. Example safe chain: neighbor overhears plan → protagonist checks original lease → unpaid balance and unusual signature → bank message → direct conversation with witness → notarial appointment → written boundary notice. Do not fabricate impossible proof.
7. **SEVEN-PART BLUEPRINT**: every Part has 5–8 scenes with action, objective, obstacle, choice, cost, state delta, carry-forward loop and exact budget. Avoid modular episodes that can be removed without affecting the end.
8. **EARLY RETENTION SURVIVAL**: the first 5,000 codepoints must have quote/event, verifiable proof, protagonist's micro-choice and second hook; use `MASTER_OUTLINE_ENGINE.md` limits.
9. **CANON FORENSIC**: fill cast/kinship, chronological, finances/ownership, legal mechanism, physical, evidence/inventory, information and emotional-state ledgers in `CANON_LEDGER_TEMPLATE.md`.
10. **COUNTERFACTUAL PREFLIGHT**: ask "Why doesn't she leave/call/help sooner?", "why doesn't he stop paying?", "who can access records?", "how long does a transfer/inheritance/legal action plausibly take?", "why would a child have the nerve?", "if the key clue is removed, could the plot still proceed?" Answer concretely.
11. **DIGNITY + CLOSURE**: ending must show new, sustainable boundary by action; allow reconciliation only after cost, accountability and changed behavior. A quiet independent life is valid.
12. **OUTLINE PERSISTENCE**: save actual exact `OUTLINE.txt` UTF-8 LF, compute real SHA256; `title_sha12` and `video_id` bound as in shared contract. No file access = label unverified and do not claim Step3 identity-proof ready.

## Narrative quality prohibitions
- No suddenly discovered account worth millions without preceding life history and document trail.
- No magical signature or automatic expulsion. When ownership is contested, show consultation, notice, waiting, evidence and consequences; do not prescribe laws for all Mexico states.
- No universal villain speech announcing the scheme to the protagonist; use imperfect, motivated clues.
- No opening with a 2,000-character biography after an exciting first line; contextualize biography during action.
- No looping same insult for 15k chars; each scene changes what somebody knows, owes, decides or fears.
- No fake immediate forgiveness because video ending is near.
- No filler mini-twists, random vehicles, unrelated rescues, miraculous pregnancy/identity unless title requires and set up.
- Dialogue in es-MX, polite and intimate forms stable according to relationship, education and age.
