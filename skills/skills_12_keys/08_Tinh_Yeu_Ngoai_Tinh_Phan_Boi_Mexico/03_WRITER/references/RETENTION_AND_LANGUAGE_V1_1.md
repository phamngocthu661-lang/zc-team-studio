# WRITER QUALITY UPGRADE V1.1 — AUTHENTIC ES-MX + HIGH RETENTION

## Scene progression
For each 8–12 minutes, introduce a consequential change (new access, altered power, relationship fracture, cost, decision) rather than another exposition of the same contract. Each of seven PARTs has distinct emotional texture. After factual scene ask: What did Valeria lose/gain in relationships that she did not already know? If none, shorten or merge.

## Proof cross-check before draft ends
Maintain a witness arrival clock **separate from** narrator's order of telling. If a source arrives after a speech, they cannot be first-hand witness unless an earlier entrance is confirmed; testimony can instead be about viewing a recording. Double-check speaker, time, room and physical visibility in P1/P6. Reconcile chapter bridge and weekdays after every A/B/C handoff.

## Spanish QA preflight by Writer (before independent QA)
- Read each sentence aloud as es-MX: eliminate mechanical calques, duplicated phrases, ambiguous pronouns and odd referents.
- Examples of avoidable issues: `modas de moda` => `tendencias pasajeras`/`las modas`; `La supe reconocer` after referring to an affair => specify `Reconocí esa cercanía...`.
- Distinguish language errors from plausible colloquial Mexico usage; do not flatten character voices into Spain Spanish.
- Avoid artificial philosopher's summaries at the end of each dialogue. Keep sensory detail that drives action and authentic kitchen/workplace atmosphere.
- If only two restaurants exist, distinguish `sede original` and `nueva sucursal` consistently.

## Length and honesty
12,500–14,500 measured spoken lexical words and 75,000–100,000 body chars. Outline center target ~13,200–13,800 words to avoid scraping the minimum. Never pad with redundant memories for length. Part and stage targets are guidance, not hard quotas that supersede overall gate. TTS actual runtime cannot be claimed without playback timing.
