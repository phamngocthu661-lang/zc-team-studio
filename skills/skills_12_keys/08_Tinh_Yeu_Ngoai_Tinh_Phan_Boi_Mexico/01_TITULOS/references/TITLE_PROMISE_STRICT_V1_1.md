# TITLE PROMISE CHECK V1.1 — MEXICO

This checklist is mandatory even if title already came from the user.

1. Write a **literal promise ledger**: (a) who says/does what, (b) object with causal meaning, (c) when it first appears, (d) how protagonist discovers it, (e) what the object can and cannot prove.
2. Explicitly distinguish **forgetting a document** vs **intentionally ignoring it**. “Olvidó un contrato” can be a rhetorical hook only when story explains what its existence frustrates; do not promise a surprise absent from the audio.
3. Show betrayal visibly/audibly in the first 60–90s, without claiming ownership transfers or legal victory just because someone makes a speech.
4. Before LOCK compare the title promise with the planned P1 reveal, midpoint turn, P6/P7 payoff. If they cannot be traced, revise **before title lock**.
5. Titles should be distinct from the supplied catalog in actors, object, setting, causal reversal, consequence, not merely synonyms. Record exact-title duplicate check and a brief premise fingerprint.
6. Do not infer measured CTR from editorial scores. A title score >=97 is not empirical success.
