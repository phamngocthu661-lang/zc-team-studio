# TITLE DNA ENGINE — MEXICO (read before S1)

## Source DNA patterns vs copy
`ÉL LA HUMILLÓ EN UNA GALA → ELLA ERA LA AUTORA/PROPIETARIA` is only a broad mechanism. Change heroine's expertise, proof, who learns it, antagonist plan, chronology and consequences to ensure original work. `MI ESPOSO ELIGIÓ A SU EX DURANTE MI PARTO → ME FUI Y...` can become a different relationship priority in another credible context without reciting an actual source script.

## Six families and novel title skeletons
F1 GALA: "En [evento], mi [pareja] [humillación concreta]; cuando [prueba material], [nuevo conflicto]". Make public status credible: award, portfolio, signed work, company board; a single guest cannot arbitrarily transfer control.
F2 MEDICAL: "Mientras [condición de salud], él [ausencia/engaño]; [decisión activa] cambió [hecho comprobable]". No spouse orders doctor to kill mother, no magic hospital rescue.
F3 FIRST LOVE: "Mi [pareja] eligió a [su ex/amiga] cuando [crisis]; yo [límite] sin saber que [evidencia]".
F4 FINANCE: "Dijo que [divorcio/venta] era una formalidad; al revisar [documento], encontré [contradicción]". Must show signature/ownership/shareholder mechanism in story, not magic wealth transfers.
F5 FAMILY: "Me quitaron mi sitio en [cena/familia]; [prueba de sacrificio] cambió [relación]". Avoid petty cruelty solely for shock.
F6 QUIET EXIT: "Él pensó que siempre lo esperaría; aquella [noche/mañana], [decisión irreversible]". A new partner need not be revenge instrument.

## Specificity checklist
- WHO cheated / betrayed WHOM? What did protagonist actually see/hear?
- What relationship makes it hurt? No interchangeable people.
- Why is protagonist empowered rather than lucky?
- What can be shown in a thumbnail as one scene?
- Does title *withhold* final resolution but not all substance?
- What material event happens on camera/audio within first 90s?

## Anti-clickbait and ethics
Never recommend: deliberate injury as audience reward, revenge-porn distribution, blackmail sex, child torture, race slurs, pregnant heroine harmed for spectacle, medically nonsensical DNR decisions, instant unauthorized property confiscation, cold CEO omnipotence, "me hice millonaria de la noche a la mañana" with no setup. Emotional intensity must arise from consequential choices.
