# PRE-FLIGHT: EVIDENCE, WITNESSES, AND EMOTIONAL PAYOFF — V1.1

## Witness temporal chain (mandatory)
For EACH witness create a row: arrival timestamp, precise room/door/field of view, statement time, what they could **hear/see first hand**, what they only learned later, disclosure path. Especially validate a witness whose entrance is described **after** a public announcement but who later claims to have heard the announcement. Reconcile both sentences before outline lock. Check video/camera start, recording custody, and subsequent sharing.

## Hard state checks
- Sites: if the venture has two total sites, use “sede original” and “segunda sede/nueva sucursal”. “Segunda sucursal” may imply a third location; define terminology in exact-number ledger.
- Day 0 and Day 7/14: compute weekday using relative-day arithmetic, distinguish inclusive/exclusive day counting; schedule every meeting in PART 4–6.
- Investor letter: intent is not equity transfer; ownership, trademark, lease, bank account, and operating contracts are distinct objects.
- A scene's quote must be sourced to a person actually present. If testimony depends on a video, say they viewed the video rather than claiming direct hearing.
- Story bible must record whose signature a document bears, whether protagonists knew about it, and whether claim “forgot” means literally forgot or ignored implications.

## Dramatic gain without padding
Use an emotional-consequence map by PART: P1 humiliation + immediate cost; P2 relational fracture; P3 first proof and unexpected resistance; P4 material price; P5 irreversible difficult choice; P6 public correction proportional to proof; P7 changed behavior. At least one scene per PART must change the hero's relationship options, not only reconfirm paperwork. Avoid serial lawyer/accountant meetings with no new stakes. Keep scenes 45–60 only when each has a distinct causal delta; never pad to hit a scene count.

## Outline gate
Every one of the 7 parts must have a hook, a newly established fact/choice, its emotional consequence, a witness/time proof, and a handoff to next Part. Any missing hard causal link blocks S2 irrespective of nominal score.
