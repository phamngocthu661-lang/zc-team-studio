# Thumbnail DNA — Cuentos del Viejo Campo sample-based, without copying

## Observed visual grammar

Top 24 sample thumbnails: single cinematic rural tableau, 1–3 principal faces with clear emotional contrast; warm sunset/ochre/dusty browns, real texture in old clothes, colonial/earthen ranch houses, stone path, horse, corn/cafetales/old house. Subject left/right readable on mobile; simple one-time event; 16:9. Some sample thumbnails show more children in the same scene and strong hand/prop gestures. Avoid generic gold arrows, circles or split collage if not reference DNA.

## Story-first character continuity

Build `VISUAL_CHARACTER_LOCK`: name, age bracket, hair, facial weathering, dress fabric/color, hat/rebozo, condition, injury. Setting: e.g. Sierra de Veracruz 1933 is not a northern desert hacienda by default. Only show actual scene's people/objects. Every A/B/C thumbnail can change camera angle but cannot invent pregnancy, child, banknote, gun, treasure or newspaper.

## Three differentiated variants

**A — SOCIAL CONTRAST / MORAL CHOICE:** medium-wide two characters eye contact, one carries burden; visible decision at entrance to rancho/hacienda. Key: readable gesture/hesitation, 2 faces max plus necessary children.

**B — COMPASSION / FAMILY:** tighter shot, child's hand / bread / water / blanket, physical problem and an adult's choice. Use visual softness without washing out emotions.

**C — PROOF / REVELATION:** portrait + exact relevant prop (old letter, borrowed key, empty bowl, repaired well, debt ledger), another person reacting. Do not visually reveal true identity if first-half script hides it; use proof object shown earlier instead.

## Prompt formula

`16:9 landscape cinematic photoreal still, Mexican rural [region] [year], era-accurate adobe hacienda/ranch, [scene from PART n], CHARACTER LOCK [ages, clothes, props], two focal emotional expressions, precise gesture, single decisive object, warm natural golden-hour side light, 35mm or 50mm lens, authentic woven textiles and dust, deep storytelling, sharp readable main faces, natural skin and hands, no montage, no split-screen, no graphic text, no modern cars/phones, no fantasy treasure, no distorted anatomy, no watermark, no unrelated extra people`.

If needing headline text, write a separate overlay brief for editor (Spanish max 2–5 words), not force unreliable AI typography.

## Quality gate 20

- Fact-accurate identities/props (6)
- One emotional question visible in 1 second (5)
- Human expressions and pose (3)
- Mexico year/region environment accurate (3)
- Mobile readability / shot separation (3)
Reject thumbnails scoring <18/20, revise **prompt** pre-publication (not a script repair; does not reset one-repair rule).
