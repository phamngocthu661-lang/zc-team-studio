# MEXICO V1 — LOGIC + ORTHOGRAPHY FORENSIC RUBRIC

## 30 Logic checks (sample evidence required)

### Cause & decision
1. Character acts from knowledge actually acquired.
2. Character has a believable motive and cost to decision.
3. Complication arises from established relationship and stakes.
4. Unexpected reveal seeded earlier; no ghost witness or miraculous signature.
5. Antagonist has method/limitations; cannot appear anywhere at will.
6. Hero changes fortune through earned effort/choice.
7. Family reconciliation costs something; forgiveness not mandatory.
8. Deception consequences are acknowledged.
9. Title question answered directly.
10. Resolution does not contradict established problem.

### Canon/time/object/land
11. Age of each person consistent across years and elapsed time.
12. Count of children/siblings and whose parent stable.
13. Accident/illness recovery time credible.
14. Distance/horse/cart/train travel matches time.
15. Object/key/letter chain of custody known.
16. How and when document was written and stored explained.
17. Same land isn't simultaneously rented and inherited without transfer explanation.
18. Prices/debts/payments consistent (no unearned cash).
19. Harvest season / drought / flood consistent with region.
20. A secret cannot be told by person who died before learning it.
21. Story dates match status of family deaths/marriages.
22. No improbable legal ruling or instant notarial transfer as plot shortcut.
23. Practical repair, crop work, medical needs, food needs have real effort.
24. Protagonist's status (maid/worker/owner) changes by visible event.
25. Foreshadowed promise appears at payoff.

### Language, script integrity
26. Correct `¿... ?`, `¡... !` marks and dialogue punctuation.
27. Acute accent, diacritics: `más/mas`, `sí/si`, `él/el`, `tú/tu`, `aún/aun` contextual.
28. Agreement: singular/plural, gender of adjectives/participles, verb tense consistent.
29. Mexican Spanish idiom natural, not word-for-word English calque; accents not stereotyped.
30. Exactly 7 Parts, no boilerplate repeated and no Stage text inside TTS body.

## Scoring guide

| Category | Max | HARD case | MAJOR case | MINOR case |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| logic | 30 | title premise impossible | large unexplained causal jump | isolated transition confusing |
| canon | 22 | contradicts identity/relationship foundational | object/age/debt inconsistent | harmless descriptor |
| spanish | 22 | widespread unreadable wrong language | recurring grammar or meaning-changing misspelling | rare accent/comma typo |
| title_payoff | 12 | promised answer absent | answer weak / unearned | minor wording |
| historical | 8 | impossible institutional premise | avoidable anachronism | minor vague detail |
| technical | 6 | missing Parts / manuscript truncated | many filler repeats | sporadic awkward pacing |

**Deduction guidance:** HARD -4 to -10 in its category and block; MAJOR -2 to -5; MINOR -0 to -1. Each finding has an explicit category and deduction; never deduct more than cap or invent score without reading. Raw sum earned must equal overall score. Multiple trivial minors may remain while READY if conditions pass.

## Output accuracy

You must identify precise Part/anchor and evidence. Do not report general `bad logic` without a quote. If file not available, report `INCOMPLETE` rather than self-certifying. QA does not author replacement passages; writes instructions + acceptance tests only.
