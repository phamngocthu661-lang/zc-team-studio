# STORY BEAT ENGINE — 7 Parts — First-person 55–75 male narrator

**Part 1 (Cold open / public wound):** 0–30s same high-stakes scene promised in title. Physical gesture/specific quoted insult. Show why father doesn't instantly retaliate; withhold answer, *not* event. Flash back to grounded life in last third. `Promise #1` established.

**Part 2 (Family ledger / personal stakes):** Build care over years with 2 scenes not exposition list. Demonstrate one overlooked skill/resource with plausible reason he kept quiet. Vulnerable child/grandchild not pure pawn. First concrete irregularity; protagonist chooses to observe. End with actionable clue.

**Part 3 (Pressure + double-deception):** First deliberate information-gathering attempt fails/has cost. Relative tightens control; scene in which audience fears father will be powerless. Establish who possesses evidence, when and why. No illegal shortcuts.

**Part 4 (Midpoint partial reversal):** Discover that initial insult concealed deeper motive. Expose a false assumption and pay off earlier prop. Father obtains legitimate corroboration; raises stakes and moral price. At midpoint the old plan must change.

**Part 5 (Worst day / relational rupture):** Family accuses father of being cruel or incompetent; prove a personal loss/relationship break. Evidence feels insufficient or access blocked; narrator doubts whether justice worth losing family. Tease last witness/document earned in previous Parts.

**Part 6 (Quiet execution / public consequence):** Father enacts precisely what he can control: change his own will, withdraw *his own* support, invoke voting rights, file truthful complaint via attorney, publicly give evidence where appropriate. Timeline and legal aftermath credible. Give antagonist choice to change; avoid overnight court victory.

**Part 7 (Aftershock / humane ending):** Resolve consequence through action: repair broken relationship if genuinely earned, help bystanders, establish boundaries. Distinguish revenge vs accountability. Last image reprises cold-open prop with new meaning. CTA after narrative, optional and brief. NEVER introduce brand new secret in final minute.

Retention: hook by 150–200 spoken words, mini-reversal every 500–900 spoken words in LONG; open loops resolved in same or later Part with a specific receipt, not arbitrary sensational twist; preserve POV only what narrator could see/hear/learn.
