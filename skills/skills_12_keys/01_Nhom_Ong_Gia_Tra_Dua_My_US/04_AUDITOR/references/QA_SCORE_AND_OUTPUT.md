# Rubric and honesty rules
Proof tool uses 10 categories, total 100, with primary weight on causal and factual coherence. All rubric values must equal category max minus sum of category actionable defect deductions minus explicitly evidenced nonactionable deductions. Nonactionable points may support 98/99 READY if there are no remaining actionable fixes, but no unsupported scores.

- Title/Hook 10; Causality/Why-now 23; Timeline/Money/Knowledge 17; Legal/Authority 10; Physical/Mechanical 10; Clue/Reveal 8; Retention 5; Character Agency 5; US English Spelling/Grammar/TTS 10; Emotional Payoff 2 = 100.
- `READY` score ≥98, length/profile PASS, all hard gates PASS, zero actionable defects. If review unfinished use `CHECKPOINT_PENDING` and no READY or patch contract.
- Find exact `Pxx` anchors within Parts; don't claim all 7x14 dimensions reviewed if missing evidence. Avoid vague 'needs better plot' diagnosis. Include exact names, dates, legal constraints, acceptance criteria.
- First audit original only freezes patch zones. Second audit after Step5 may freeze final status but NEVER a second edit contract. Score may decrease after discovering pre-existing misses; do not boost artificially.

## V1.1 scoring boundary
Keep all 10 categories, weights, QA JSON schema, 98 threshold and saturation evidence exactly as before for backward compatibility. `CREATIVE_READOUT` is a separately labeled human editorial observation, not a new 11th score category and not automatic Step5 authorization. No '99/100 implies better than competitors' claim without retention/CTR evidence.
