# PASS/FAIL checks — focus continuity, logic and spelling

## Logical chain
- P1 title promised action actually occurs with earned mechanism? Narrator's information access possible? Clear why the father waited? Any plot resolution rely on unnamed lawyer, secret camera, or government record conjured without setup?
- Person: Father can't know private conversations unless later told and source is named. Children cannot learn secret father's plan before disclosure without observable communication.
- Temporal: birth/age, adoption, marriage years, travel hours, court timelines, business days, transfer windows, criminal investigation/due process, written deadlines.
- Finances: bank account owner, trust grantor/trustee/beneficiary roles, retirement savings, escrow, company shares, spending authorization, landlord/tenant, bills. Never confuse paying mortgage with sole ownership or inheriting with already owning.
- Mechanics: physical path, keys, locks, tech, recording and CCTV access, message sender/receiver, proof document signatures.
- Emotional: why narrator chooses restraint, why relative acts, what's risked, whether aftermath shows growth or consequences.

## American English proofreading
- Apostrophe and dialogue quote pairing (Dad’s vs Dads, “you’re” vs “your”); tense changes, subject–verb, pronoun relationships, unintentional name swaps; two consecutive duplicate words, collapsed sentences, missing spaces, mistaken homophones; capitalization of names/states/brands; consistent spellings `color`, `center`, `favorite`, `canceled`, `realize`; punctuation supporting TTS breath units and understandable attribution.
- Do not 'fix' a grammatically nonstandard character voice if intentional and comprehensible. Do not remove idiomatic contractions indiscriminately.
- Transcript-derived style fragments such as `the room went silent`, `they went pale` are not spelling errors. Mark optional stylistic suggestions separately, not as mandatory patch defects.

## Severity
P0: script incompleteness, illegal/physically impossible premise, fundamental contradiction. P1: causal/money/knowledge/legal failure damaging credibility. P2: major inconsistent fact or confusing dialogue. P3: real localized grammar/spelling issue. Aggregate multiple same-cause typos in one patch zone when feasible, but every edit must be frozen beforehand.

## Postrepair
Only one read-only final Step4; classify remaining defects; no second Step5 or multi-round improvement cycle.

## V1.1 editorial separation
Also generate non-patch `CREATIVE_READOUT` under `CREATIVE_READOUT_V1_1.md`. Failure to dramatize an event explicitly promised by TITLE can be an objective hard-gate issue; making a truthful resolution less thrilling is NOT an automatic Pxx. Never add aesthetic paragraphs to the single authorized repair contract.
