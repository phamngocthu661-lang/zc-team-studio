# STEP6 PROMISE-MATCH SEO / THUMBNAIL V1.1

The primary locked title is not negotiable. All SEO hypotheses must derive from the current SHA-bound QA READY script; do not 'solve' a weak climax by depicting a new spectacle in a thumbnail or trailer.

## Audience promise match
For each packaging item state `SCRIPT_PART`, `ACTUAL_SCENE`, `VIEWER_EXPECTATION`, `REVEAL_VISIBILITY`, `TRUTH_CHECK`. The thumbnail must be visually readable and show a real humiliation, evidence, or consequence that *actually occurs*. If the real event is an attorney letter and subsequent buyer withdrawal, don't show a courthouse arrest or a dramatic confrontation that never happened.

## A/B/C hypotheses grounded in distinct payoffs
- **A: wound** — moment of insult/public exclusion with father emotion and distinct prop.
- **B: mechanism** — precise real object/witness/action that earns reversal; don't show secrets before they were discovered.
- **C: consequence** — documented/acted-upon reversal with another character reaction; no photoshop implying illegal eviction, instant seizure or forced inheritance.

For the top candidate, use high legibility and one short truth-based emotional overlay; 3–8 words maximum. An image is a static creative work, not evidence of CTR improvement. Report expected hypothesis and actual future measurement plan (CTR by impressions source, first-30s retention, avg view duration), do not claim superiority from internal score.

## Trailer
25 seconds is okay, but only 3–5 real beats from first 6 Parts and no payoff fabrication. Distinguish *teaser* from finale; no footage of new incidents or unsupported police/legal action. End in a real central dilemma, not false cliffhanger.
