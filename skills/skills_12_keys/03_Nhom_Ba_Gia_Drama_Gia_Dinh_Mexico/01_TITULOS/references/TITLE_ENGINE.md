# TITLE SYSTEM — 12 specific rules and scoring

1. Input: source title, list of titles or original premise. Use available title-performance data descriptively, without claiming causal CTR. Rank primarily within each supplied channel to avoid mixing channel baselines.
2. Identify one **family violation**; one powerful concrete scene (cumpleaños, llaves, boda, recibos, escritura, cocina); one central protagonist; one truthful unfinished question.
3. Form a causal promise: `Mi [familiar] hizo/dijo [specific betrayal] — [my specific first action / object / condition]...` or a quoted offense `«...», dijo mi nuera. Entonces...`.
4. Generate exactly 5 distinct approaches (not synonym rotations): defiant, quietly clever, investigative, bittersweet, social-public accountability.
5. Default title <=100 Unicode codepoints including whitespace and punctuation; title can be clipped to 100 only via **rewrite**, never blind truncation.
6. Source DNA preservation refers to emotional contract, not phrase or plot copying. Require change in underlying evidence chain and reversal, not just replacing names.
7. Avoid misleading threat (`mi hija me mató`) for social embarrassment; don't claim legal powers unavailable to ordinary people.
8. Prioritize Mexican speech patterns over English punctuation/ALL CAPS. Up to 2 emphasized words only when they help.
9. Never state documented true story unless verified.
10. Score on hook clarity 20, audience fit 15, emotional specificity 20, promised payoff feasibility 20, originality 15, es-MX fluency 10.
11. Don't mechanically invent 98/100: record reason, uncertainty and if ceiling lower than target report it.
12. Lock exactly ONE winner; other four remain nonbinding. Only the locked winner flows to outline.

### Original title examples (NOT copied from the channels)
- `«Tu habitación ya está ocupada», dijo mi nuera. Abrí el cajón donde guardaba las escrituras`
- `Mi hijo cobró la renta de mi casa durante un año. Lo descubrí por un recibo olvidado`
- `En mis 70 años dejaron una silla vacía para mí. El mesero me entregó otra cuenta`
Each example is only a title; subsequent script must prove its promise causally.

## V1.1 anti-overpromise test
Before locking each title, write exactly one plausible first-5-minute scene and the real-life action that could resolve the title promise in Part 6. Reject a catchy title when its legal/payoff mechanism cannot be causally earned. Optimize distinct *emotional mechanisms*, not just synonyms; do not transform an elderly woman into a billionaire as a universal reveal. In Step 2 ensure every title promise has an evidence ID and a dated narrator knowledge path.
