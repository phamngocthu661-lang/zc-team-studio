# STATE-LOCK / US REALISM CHECKS

State/region, narrator address, service branch/rank, child age/guardianship, court jurisdiction, asset owner, source and accessibility of evidence, bank/credit agreement, marital/separate ownership, timeline of filing/service/hearings and medical consent must be assigned in outline. If legal outcome varies, show a lawyer explaining conditional possibility rather than confident nationwide legal rule.

Checklist: 1) Who legally owns/controls asset? 2) Who knew fact and when? 3) What action created a record? 4) Where did record come from and can narrator see it? 5) Which authority has jurisdiction? 6) What elapsed time did procedure require? 7) Why did she not act sooner? 8) Did a villain counter with proportionate realistic means? 9) What irreversible cost does heroine pay? 10) Does ending literally fulfil locked title?
