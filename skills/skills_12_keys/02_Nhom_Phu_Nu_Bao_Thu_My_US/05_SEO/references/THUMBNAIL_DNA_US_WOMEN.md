# THUMBNAIL SYSTEM — BENCHMARK ANALYSIS WITHOUT COPYING

Observed competitor motifs: Iron Rose thumbnails often have a large first-person text block on black occupying ~55–65% width with photo of strong/sad adult woman to right, occasionally in US uniform/at bedside. Quiet Triumphs often use high contrast red/yellow/green story excerpts plus a female near ER, kitchen, bank, paperwork, confrontations. These are descriptions of sample thumbnails, not evidence of CTR. Originality rule: change composition/shot style/focal props and never reuse identical character photo/text.

Design 3 tests:
A. Face: clean closeup of protagonist, one concrete wound to trust (broken photo, missing chair, silent phone), ~2–6 word text.
B. Evidence: close visible credible date/receipt/deed/voicemail screen; protagonist observing from side; 2–8 word text; avoid invented IDs or realistic false legal documents with specific private data.
C. Reversal: contrast between villain shocked in depth and calm heroine in foreground with one authentic asset/letter/legal scene; 2–9 word text.

16:9 1920x1080 intent, cinematic photorealistic, healthy light/contrast, readable small-screen typography, no gore, no military insignia/rank claims beyond story. Each thumbnail must depict an actual story moment, not generic soldier/handcuffs. If rendering not available, state prompts only.
