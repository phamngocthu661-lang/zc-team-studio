# US REALISM SOURCE GATE V1.1 — CHECK THE EXACT JURISDICTION

This is a *research protocol for fiction*, not legal advice, and not a one-size-fits-all catalogue of court outcomes. Pin state, relevant year, court stage, and remedy before using legal rules. Prefer legislature, federal code, judicial branch or federal agencies; record URL, date checked, the relevant concept, ambiguity and how the scene fits.

## Military parent / custody example (Wisconsin, NOT a nationwide template)
- Wisconsin Stat. §767.451 distinguishes substantial changes in placement from minor changes and includes a branch for substantially equal placement orders. Do not infer an automatic change of schedule solely from proving one parent's misquotation. Source: https://law.justia.com/codes/wisconsin/chapter-767/section-767-451/ (confirm current official Wisconsin legislature text before relying on exact sections).
- Federal 50 U.S.C. §3938 addresses deployment and temporary custody orders; deployment absence may not be the *sole* factor in a permanent custody-modification best-interest analysis. Source: https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t50/s3938/ . This is not an automatic military mother victory. Distinguish National Guard status (state vs federal active duty) and whether that federal provision applies to specific service status.
- 50 U.S.C. §3932 may permit stay on the terms provided there; do not invent a universal postponement or soldiers' absolute immunity. Source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title50/html/USCODE-2024-title50-chap50-subchapII-sec3932.htm .
- FERPA generally provides educational-record rights to both parents absent a binding limitation; school custodian access and a teacher's firsthand observations are different evidentiary categories. Source: https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/case-divorce-do-both-parents-have-rights-under-ferpa . Don't imply a teacher can always disclose every child record to a third party.

## Other fiction gates
- Banking: classify joint owner, authorized user, signer, beneficiary; bank policy and applicable state law determine access. A screenshot does not automatically prove crime or entitlement.
- Property: inspect actual deed form, recording, marital/community-property implications, liens, signatures and title-search timeline, not internet rumors about what is 'mine'.
- Military: specify rank/role, duty type, chain of command authority and civilian jurisdiction. Commander cannot adjudicate a divorce, arrest an ex, or order a county court.
- Court: identify requested remedy; standing; filing; service; notice; evidence objections; hearing; possible written orders; calendar time and appeal. Avoid instant criminal sentencing at family-court hearings.

If state unclear or source missing for *outcome-determinative* legal claim, mark `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`, leave alternatives, and DO NOT lock that scene as legally certain. Distinguish invented scenes from real sources; never fabricate case law, citations, exhibits or court forms.
