# TITLE-PROMISE-OWNER GATE V1.1 (STEP1)
Purpose: prevent titles whose named promised person, object, or reversal is replaced by unrelated paperwork late in the story. This is a creative preflight, not a score computed by a script.

For **each** title candidate produce a three-cell mini contract:
- `TITLE_EXPLICIT_PROMISE`: what exactly must be true by the ending, stated in ordinary US English.
- `PAYOFF_OWNER`: the person/object expressly credited by the title (teacher, soldier, grandmother, deed). That owner MUST provide a non-interchangeable step in the reversal, not merely stand in the courtroom while someone else provides all proof.
- `OWNED_EVIDENCE`: original, accessible item which **that** owner kept/produced or authenticated; distinguish personal handwritten observation from official agency records.
- `CAUSE_TO_OUTCOME`: the protagonist uses the evidence to request a plausible action; legal decisions require appropriate procedure and independent considerations.
- `PAYOFF_SCENE`: Part number (usually 6) and visible action which fulfills title literally.

### Hard rejection
Reject even a very clickable candidate if the named source of evidence can be removed with no change to outcome; if title implies automatic win from one document; if a judge, teacher, bank or commander takes an implausible action; or if ending requires a twist unsupported by a chain-of-custody path.

### Example (inspired by a QA failure pattern, not reusing a story)
Title naming a child's teacher: teacher's contemporaneous dated notes, personally witnessed discrepancies and testified-to contextual observations must contribute a **distinct fact**. School registrar's properly accessible attendance records may corroborate but cannot replace the promised teacher's contribution. Legal result still requires jurisdiction and the correct substantive standard.

Output additionally: `PROMISE_OWNER_GATE: PASS/REPLAN`, `NON_SUBSTITUTABLE_PAYOFF`, `OUTCOME_BASIS`, `PROHIBITED_SHORTCUT`.
A PASS is editorial, not proof of real records or law.
