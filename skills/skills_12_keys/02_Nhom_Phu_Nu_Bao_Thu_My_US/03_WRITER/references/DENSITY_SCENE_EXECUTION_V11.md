# DENSITY / SCENE EXECUTION V1.1 — STEP3 A/B/C

The writer does NOT create drama by repeating evidence summaries, doubling inner monologues or running several equivalent lawyer conversations. Follow locked outline and companion preflight. The primary audience promise is the reason to keep watching; every Part must advance its *specific causal chain*.

## Scene drafting mini-card (apply privately before each scene)
- `BEFORE_STATE`: what the heroine objectively wants/knows/controls immediately before this scene.
- `TRIGGER`: who does something that cannot be ignored now.
- `CHOICE`: narrator makes a concrete choice within her authority, with visible action/dialogue.
- `NEW_FACT`: discovery not already stated twice; classify as allegation, testimony, original record, inference, or verified fact.
- `AFTER_STATE`: specific options, risk, relationship or asset control changed from BEFORE_STATE.
- `COST`: money, time, safety, trust or strategic setback.
- `FORWARD`: one unresolved precise question; no artificial 'you won't believe' tease.
If BEFORE_STATE and AFTER_STATE are equivalent -> combine/cut scene. If heroine does not act in multiple consecutive scenes -> add justified choice without inventing authority.

## Compression without losing emotion
- Max two full retellings of any pivotal exhibit; later mentions must introduce authentication, contradiction, real use or consequence. Reducing repetitive legal advice dialogue is preferable to adding dramatic nonsequitur.
- Place a causal turn approximately each 400–700 words, but organic dialogue scenes may be shorter/longer. In 13k+ words, tension must be proven through changing stakes, not arbitrary suspense punctuation.
- Ground at most one signature sensory detail per scene and make it causally relevant.
- Do not reveal legal outcome before hearing, treat school staff statements as a court judgment, or state that a witness can 'win custody'. Final relief must have separately planted evidentiary/legal basis.

## Promise specificity for named witnesses
If title credits a teacher, parent, neighbor, doctor, deed or soldier, that owner must make an irreplaceable difference at the payoff. Supporting official records can corroborate, but may not displace the named promise. Identify **which firsthand observation** belongs to that witness vs what a registrar, attorney, or judge can properly verify.

## Part-specific signals
P1: betrayal and her first costly choice. P2: credible constraint and investigative permission. P3: a small consequential gain and new independent verification. P4: midpoint contradiction that narrows her choices. P5: real opponent counter with procedural/legal cost. P6: proof chain presented through credible steps with rebuttal opportunity and precise title payoff. P7: limited earned result, residual cost, a decision proving identity change.

### STOP/REPLAN
If the outline lacks credible scene deltas to sustain 70k–80k characters, STOP **before** saving any new stage and report the outline deficiency. Don't fix it by padding or overwriting previously frozen A/B/C. If outside STEP3, follow controller; no silent additional tool calls or stages.
