# TITLE ENGINE & RUBRIC V1.0

## Six title family frames
A: `[Age + ordinary rank] + [specific inconvenient accident with an adult woman] + [respectful unusual request] + [consequence]`
B: `[Lone man's modest place] + [adult woman judged by others] + [small concrete help] + [life changes]`
C: `[Workplace says man is useless] + [observable special skill + difficult decision] + [reversal with cost]`
D: `[Omiai/old reunion] + [awkward condition] + [woman's surprising response] + [return encounter]`
E: `[Transferred or relocated] + [isolated community + adult woman] + [human contribution] + [home]`
F: `[Housing conflict/trust] + [hidden reason] + [revealed through behavior] + [mutual choice]`

Always vary the trigger, power balance, real-life job details, woman's independent problem, setting, money and ending. Do not make every heroine `美人社長` or every hero an undercover president.

## Score editorial 100
- Character and target viewer empathy (20)
- Specific opening situation and stakes (20)
- Curiosity without explaining twist (20)
- Plausibility and story-serviceability (15)
- Freshness relative to dataset (15)
- Natural short Japanese & <=100 characters (10)
No auto-pass through number alone; final check on promise-to-payoff and absence of exploitative bait.

- `53歳で島の図書館に飛ばされた俺。毎週返却される白紙の手紙が、無口な40歳女性の願いを教えてくれた` (draft example)
- `44歳の俺が見合いで「家事は苦手です」と告白すると、39歳の彼女は意外な条件を出してきた` (draft example)
Use actual selected output, not the examples as default.

## Output checklist
1. Japanese punctuation natural (句読点, 「」, …); spellcheck gender/age role. 2. No false superpower/payoff. 3. Hook grounds next ~30s. 4. Test premise can sustain 7 Parts without filler. 5. Count codepoints including punctuation and spaces, not UTF-8 byte count. 6. Do not write the final spoiler into title. 7. If source has explicit age or number, new alternative must stay truthful to new story.


## V1.1 title-to-payoff proof
Before selecting winner, build 3-line `PROMISE_PROOF`: (1) visible opening action, (2) concrete consequence to single male lead if he chooses to help/refuse, (3) **reveal changing a decision or relationship**, not only a benign secret. If the headline says `誰にも見せなかった計画` and the reveal is merely a zoning sketch that no one has seen, ask whether it emotionally justifies the headline. Promote specificity while respecting plausibility: the plan can contain an intimate choice, sacrifice or requirement, but no manufactured billionaire/estate secret. Require one manga-thumbnail frame from Part1 and Part4 that the title fairly describes. No plot copied from reference dataset. Do not score above 90 if there is only procedural intrigue and no protagonist personal cost.
