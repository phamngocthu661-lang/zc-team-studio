For each: (1) `ID/Family/Title es-MX` <100 Python codepoints including spaces; (2) exact count `len(title)` if code tool exists; (3) one-sentence opening betrayal; (4) father's leverage and why morally/logically his; (5) a twist that has been seeded; (6) novelty vs corpus; (7) high-stakes payoff not supplied wholesale in title; (8) one line demonstrating specific Mexican local context; (9) viable 7-Part FULL 70k–80k (never half-length) (enough decisions and consequences).
