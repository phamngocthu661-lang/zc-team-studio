# SEVEN PART ROMANCE OUTLINE ENGINE V1.1
1. Hook / involuntary encounter: credible practical stakes + the intimate question (850–1,000 words SOFT).
2. Mutual reason to continue: observable chemistry and individual goals (950–1,100 SOFT).
3. First true exposure: one unexpected vulnerable disclosure; reaction changes choice (1,000–1,150 SOFT).
4. Midpoint voluntary bond: first explicit choice; increase professional/personal costs (1,050–1,200 SOFT).
5. Real cost and setback: grounded consequence of flaw not random villain (1,050–1,200 SOFT).
6. Repair through effort: accountability + independent agency + changing action (1,000–1,150 SOFT).
7. Earned couple decision: trustworthy proof, unique motif echo, future still realistic (1,100–1,250 SOFT).
All part figures are SOFT PLANNING BANDS only. Adjust 7 individual budgets to fit the agreed GLOBAL target; do not pretend the reference durations established exact written words.

For each beat: trigger > on-page observable action > second character's actual response > new state > later payoff link. Track FACT vs BELIEF; make revelations traceable. Avoid repetitive virtue speeches, forced plot coincidence, long exposition blocks, easy fixes, generic analogies, cosmetic obstacles. Cover realistic American place transitions, work call decisions, travel, ages, ex-partner history, schedules and finances.

Tension audit: At ~10% has he risked anything socially? ~25% have both shown a choice? ~50% is relationship transformed? ~70–80% is there credible cost? Last ~10% is affection proven by action? These are editor checks, not rigid timestamps; use only where story fit improves.
