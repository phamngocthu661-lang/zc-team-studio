# Spanish Mexico SEO pack — intention and responsible intrigue

## Description structure
Start with exact celebrity name and documented decisive event in the first 160 characters. 1 question but no insinuation without sourcing. Explain conflict without excessive adjectives, acknowledge counterview if relevant. Add 1 paragraph framing career/legacy, one "qué sabemos y qué no" where source conflicts, and clear primary-source references (URL, outlet, publication date). End CTA inviting discussion of public work or decisions, not private gossip.

## Keyword groups
- Intent discovery: historia de [Nombre], biografía de [Nombre], documental de [Nombre], trayectoria de [Nombre], qué pasó con [Nombre].
- Genre: documental biográfico, historia de famosos, farándula mexicana, cultura pop mexicana, ídolos de México, detrás de los aplausos.
- Event-specific only if verified: canción/novela/entrevista/año/documented interview.
- Tags do not grant a right to assert an alleged offense, secret affair or illness.

## Metadata scoring /100
Source/title fidelity 35, language 15, first-two-lines hook 15, distinct audience discoverability 15, thumbnail synergy without misinformation 15, mobile clarity 5. 95+ desired, no publishing on factual mismatch regardless numeric score.

## Supporting titles
Never displace primary locked title. Use one alternate centered on event and one on the public career impact; Spanish es-MX and ≤100 characters.
