# Japanese audio-only 60+/70+ voice writing guide

## Micro-story grammar (not rigid template)
Each review must have (a) concrete before: expectation, (b) lived scene, (c) observed price/word/look/task/obstacle, (d) cost and decision, (e) one earned recognition or fresh conflict. Let it end with distinctive aftertaste, not same explicit lesson. A 60+ voice can be dignified, funny, irritated, proud, relieved; don't write 30 helpless victims.

The listener cannot see punctuation. Keep short paragraphs 1–3 sentences, clear identity when voices switch, natural `です・ます` at narration level, conversational `ね/よ` varies person. Respect dialect only if meaningful and not caricature. Numbers: use clear yen/age/month references, date if needed, consistent arithmetic. Kanji: `再雇用`, `再就職`, `在職老齢年金`, `シルバー人材センター`, `年金受給`, `ハローワーク`, `公営住宅`, `扶養` correctly; avoid convoluted legal jargon and unexplained acronyms.

Avoid 1:1 translation of competitor's English title (CSV titles may be English-translated metadata), avoid fictional testimony presented as interviews. Avoid unverifiable tax/pension rules, impossibly precise costs, impossible public housing allocation claims. No guaranteed income or legal advice.

### Anti-repetition
No more than 3 stories with same ending, 3 similar surprise structures, or 2 identical protagonist names. At least 6 helpful/agency cases, 5 light cases, 5 contradictory outcomes. No excessive `衝撃`, `地獄`, `まさか`, `涙が止まらない` throughout narration. Concrete numbers need logic, not sensationalism.

## V1.1 — voice fingerprint + anti-formula writing
The first run with 30 stories was coherent, but its characteristic danger was excessive `manager consultation → successful adjustment → lesson` closure. Make sure not every speaker ends with a perfectly solved problem. Put at least one narrative beat in mid-scene with spoken dialogue or physical evidence instead of a summary. Avoid ending many stories with a formula such as `近いだけで...` or `...ということが大事`. In each voice vary rhythm, irony, dignity, regret, hard choices and the presence/absence of explicit advice. Balance admissions of mistakes with at least four joyful unexpected outcomes. Prefer concrete sensory or measurable consequence to generic moral. Writer must cross-check outline canon for each speaker before saving A/B/C. **Do not modify the locked canon in the draft**. Default target 11.8–12.6k voiced chars with flexible budget allocation; 10.5–13.5k remains legal technical range.
