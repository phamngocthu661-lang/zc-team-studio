# 7 PART × 30 REVIEWS — FOCUSED 30–40 MIN EDITORIAL ARCHITECTURE

| PART | Reviews | Editorial purpose | Energy |
| 1 | 01–04 | Contradiction upfront; specific first-hand moment | immediate surprise |
| 2 | 05–08 | Money & hidden practical conditions | concern/recognition |
| 3 | 09–12 | Staff, family and power dynamic | emotional tension |
| 4 | 13–16 | Counterexamples + funny unexpected turns | release/nuance |
| 5 | 17–20 | Hidden cost that emerges over weeks/months | concern/midpoint |
| 6 | 21–25 | High-impact decisions & respectful agency | peak/resolve |
| 7 | 26–30 | Contrast, practical choices, unexpectedly hopeful last story | closure |

Every chapter begins with spoken transition at most 2–3 sentences. Do not say `続きは次の章で` mechanically. Organize by evolving *information value*, not fake chronology. Stories must be intelligible stand-alone and editable in any order without continuity holes. Use cross-references only for general ideas, never imply all 30 belong to one family/company.

### Example for topic 再雇用
01 morning train at 6:10 despite 4 days/week contract; 02 equipment fee surprised; 03 former subordinate is manager; 04 role changed to customer-facing. Ensure every event is original and credible; don't reuse these four if derivative. Complete all 30 varied decisions and moods. Avoid precise wage amounts if unchecked or if not necessary.

## V1.1: Variable emotional architecture for 30 independent stories
Before outline lock, add 30-row `STORY_SIGNATURE_MATRIX` with: number, age/persona, exact expected benefit, **one irreplaceable scene/detail**, hidden cost/benefit, who has agency, consequence in daily life, ending type (from ≥6 types), emotional tone (≥5 distinct), source-type, and 1-line promise proof. Explicitly mark `POSITIVE` / `BITTERSWEET` / `UNRESOLVED` / `HUMOR` / `LOSS` with a spread across all seven chapters. Require at least four characters with a personal choice other than "ask boss to adjust". Duplicate mechanisms across adjacent episodes are revised BEFORE outline lock. In each chapter choose one payoff review and one contrast review; place more gripping character-level events at #01, #07–08, #16–17, #25–26 and #30, without forced fake crises. Do not use false universal pension rules. 7-part packing remains 4/4/4/4/4/5/5.
