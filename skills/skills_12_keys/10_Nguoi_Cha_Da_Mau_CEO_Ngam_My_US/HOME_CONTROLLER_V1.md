## Gating sequence
1. S1: 5 distinctive candidate titles, score by grounded events, select exact title, title literal truth preflight, avoid duplicates in known ledger. Save `01_title.json`.
2. S2: approved 7-part causal outline with cast/timeline/skill proof, 90-second timed hook design (`cost/clue/question`), exactly when each mystery reveal may occur, before/after title promise, audience emotional stakes and unique evidence pathway. Save `02_outline.md` and frozen `02_hook_contract.json` with required event IDs/deadlines and reveal policy.
3. S3: full 6.5–8k-word TTS text with required events in ACTUAL first 90s; persist body+real manifest; don't pass a synopsis.
4. S4 INITIAL: independent full-text review + mechanical count/word-position evidence, defects and single frozen contract; `HIGH` hook mismatch automatically FAIL. No rewriting by auditor.
5. S5 ONLY IF REQUIRED: ONE cumulative writer revision with exact match and reverse reconstruction proof; never reopen automatically.
6. S4 FINAL (or initial direct PASS): independent entire-script QA and `04_hook_proof.json` for the **final** body; validate file/lineage via `scripts/validate_production.py`. Any fail → NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW.
7. S6: only once truly READY, exact title, SEO text, three thumbnail prompts; no invented watch-time/CTR or images unless user asks.

**Stop conditions:** Missing files, title contradiction, WPM/hook evidence mismatch, no approved 7-part outline, incomplete body, ungrounded or duplicate claim, invalid hash, out-of-scope patch, revision_count >1, post-repair HIGH defect or failed validator. Report precise cause and no fake PASS.

**Backward compatibility:** file names, states, commands and folder names remain unchanged; `04_hook_proof.json` is new V2 file. `HOME_CONTROLLER_V1.md` filename deliberately retained for installed project references.
