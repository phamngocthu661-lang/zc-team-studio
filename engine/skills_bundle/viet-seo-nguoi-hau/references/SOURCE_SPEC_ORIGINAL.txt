NẾU STORY LÀ DRAMA / DANGER
=================================

Hook cần ưu tiên:

- immediate tension;
- sudden realization;
- hostile eye contact;
- urgent body movement;
- physical danger;
- shocking reaction;
- background people reacting;
- camera push-in;
- micro-expression;
- unresolved threat.

Không giải quyết vấn đề trong 8 giây.

Video phải KẾT THÚC trong trạng thái:

"WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?"

- grief;

=================================
NẾU STORY LÀ INJUSTICE / REVENGE
=================================

Hook ưu tiên:

- humiliation;
- contempt;
- protagonist trying not to break;
- antagonist arrogance;
- shocked witnesses;
- sudden change in protagonist expression;
- beginning of reversal.

Mục tiêu tạo:

ANGER
+
DESIRE TO SEE JUSTICE.

=================================
8-SECOND HOOK STRUCTURE
=================================

Default structure:

0.0–2.0s
IMMEDIATE VISUAL HOOK

2.0–5.0s
ESCALATION / REACTION

5.0–7.0s
KEY REALIZATION / THREAT

7.0–8.0s
CUT BEFORE PAYOFF

Do not resolve the scene.

=================================
VIDEO MOTION RULES
=================================

Because the source is a thumbnail image:

Keep character identity, clothing, setting and composition consistent with the source image.

Animation must feel natural.

Use only movements appropriate to the scene, such as:

- character turns head;
- eyes widen;
- breath changes;
- hand tightens;
- one step closer;
- subtle recoil;
- tear forming;
- antagonist leans in;
- background characters react;
- camera slow push-in;
- slight handheld tension;
- subtle rack focus.

Avoid:

- random running;
- excessive movement;
- changing wardrobe;
- changing faces;
- adding unrelated objects;
- full scene transformation;
- revealing the ending.

=================================
HOOK DIALOGUE
=================================

When dialogue materially improves the hook:

Generate ONE short spoken line.

Usually:

3–10 words.

It must sound natural in US English.

The dialogue must:

- increase tension;
- create curiosity;
- expose conflict;

but NOT explain the whole story.

If silence + facial acting is stronger:

NO DIALOGUE.
