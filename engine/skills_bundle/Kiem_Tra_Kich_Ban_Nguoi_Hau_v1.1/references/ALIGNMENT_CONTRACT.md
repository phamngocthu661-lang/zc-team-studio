# ALIGNMENT CONTRACT — NGƯỜI HẦU KEY V1.1

## PURPOSE
Đây là source contract để QA không dùng rule từ key khác. Bắt buộc đọc `SHARED_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md` trước file này. Raw/historical DNA là evidence-only và không được override operational contract.

## 1. CURRENT KEY
Niche:
**Mafia Boss × Maid / Waitress / Secretary / Poor Woman / Underestimated Woman / Hidden Expert**.

Core fantasy:
- heroine bị xem thường/áp lực;
- power character nguy hiểm nhưng protective;
- heroine có agency + competence;
- respect/partnership tăng theo hành động và bằng chứng;
- climax dùng cả heroine contribution và Boss resources/power;
- payoff phải justify click.

## 2. OUTLINE CONTRACT

## 3. DNA ARCHITECTURE
### DNA A — Mafia Boss Archives
Identity: Accidental Encounter + Professional Competence + Dark Protection + Queen Coronation.
Natural map: **6 Beats**.
Heroine competence materially changes crisis; dark protection evolves toward partnership.

### DNA B — Mafia Boss Romance
Identity: Underestimated Woman + Territorial Protection + Tactical Leverage + Equal Dark Partnership.
Natural map: **5 Beats**.
Grittier physical realism; leverage repeatedly shifts toward heroine's intelligence.

### DNA C — The Mafia's Hidden Heart
Identity: Mundane Humiliation + Hidden Prodigy + Impossible Rescue/Warning + Forced Coexistence + Siege/Betrayal + Coronation.
Natural map: **5 Beats**.
Most important principle: heroine intelligence stays one step ahead of Boss violence.

Secondary influence is allowed only if outline explicitly selects it; do not create DNA soup during QA/rewrite.

## 4. DYNAMIC PART LOCK
`N = number of DETAILED OUTLINE Beats`.
Script must have exactly N Parts, 1:1 in order.
No default 8-Part correction.

## 5. SCRIPT LENGTH / WORD LOCK
- Standard target: **44,000–48,000 visible characters**.
- Standard word target: **7,400–7,650 words**.
- Soft word caution: 7,750.
- Strict word lock: **TOTAL_WORDS <8,000**; exactly 8,000 = FAIL.
- Recovery hard envelope: 40,000–55,000 characters.
- 40,000–43,999 or 48,001–55,000 = TARGET MISS unless user override.
- 55,000 hard max.
- all Parts belong to one complete script.

## 6. CURRENT WRITING NON-NEGOTIABLES
- American English.
- third-person past tense / cinematic near-omniscient unless outline explicitly locks otherwise.
- no recap at new Part openings.
- heroine agency is non-negotiable.
- competence must be seeded and shown through multi-step process when plot-resolving.
- at least one dual-pressure physical/safety + technical/intellectual sequence under full current writing contract.
- meaningful antagonists have believable motives.
- no male-lead violence/roughness/non-consensual touch against heroine.
- protection must not erase heroine agency.
- no deus ex machina.
- armed/action conflict needs complication and physical consequences.
- post-action adrenaline crash/recovery consequence when action set-piece occurs.
- threat structure follows outline; no surprise new mastermind layer.
- no forced marriage/sexual payoff outside title/outline promise.
- romance is consensual and non-explicit.

## 7. RETENTION CONTRACT
- hook activates title promise quickly;
- default hook target ~640–870 chars only if compatible with outline/DNA;
- approximately every 450–550 words on average, something meaningful should change: evidence, leverage, danger, relationship, information, choice, reversal;
- every Part advances at least one major loop;
- midpoint materially changes story state;
- climax uses established setup;
- major loops close at planned stages.

## 8. STYLE CONTRACT
- tense cinematic dark romance, not heritage/restoration prose;
- American contractions and speakable TTS rhythm;
- Boss dialogue generally short/declarative;
- heroine becomes shorter/clinical inside expertise;
- no exposition dialogue dumps;
- **zero bracketed physical/audio/stage cues** in TTS-clean script body; any `[beat]`, `[silence]`, `[breath]`, `[SFX]`, `[music]`, camera/editing/production cue is a contract defect unless the latest user explicitly requested production markup.

## 9. SCRIPT-BODY FORBIDDEN TROPE WORDS
Unless title/outline locks them as quote/name:
- fairy tale
- prince charming
- destiny
- helpless
- savior
- magic
- compromise
- ordinary

## 10. YOUTUBE-SAFE CONTRACT
Fictional/dramatized dark crime romance must not become:
- recruitment/promotion/support of real criminal organizations;
- graphic gore;
- actionable crime/weapon/evasion instructions;
- sexual violence/coercive romance;
- explicit sexual content;
- hate speech/group demonization;
- dangerous illegal instruction.
