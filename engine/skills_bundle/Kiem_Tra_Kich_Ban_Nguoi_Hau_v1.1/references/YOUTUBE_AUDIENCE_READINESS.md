# YOUTUBE + MAFIA DARK-ROMANCE READINESS V1.1

## PURPOSE
Publication-readiness layer for fictional/dramatized Mafia Boss × Maid long-form storytelling.
Do not treat this as a static banned-word list; context matters.

## 1. COMMUNITY / ADVERTISER RISK CLASSES
- **YT-P0** — likely Community Guidelines risk: must remove/reframe.
- **YT-P1** — high advertiser/monetization risk: repair unless user explicitly accepts a permitted tradeoff.
- **YT-P2** — avoidable sensitivity: safer wording/context without story loss.
- **YT-OK** — contextually appropriate fictional reference: preserve.

## 2. CRIMINAL ORGANIZATIONS
Flag/reframe:
- praise, recruitment, support, fundraising or propaganda for real violent/criminal organizations;
- calls to emulate organized crime;
- operational instructions that help wrongdoing.

Fictional/fictitious underworld characters can remain compelling; narration must not become recruitment or real-world advocacy.

## 3. VIOLENCE
Allowed in story context when non-graphic and plot-serving.
Flag:
- gore focus;
- exposed tissue/dismemberment/mutilation detail;
- prolonged torture detail;
- sadistic shock as entertainment focus;
- celebratory graphic injury.

Prefer consequence, fear, disorientation, ringing ears, shaking, nausea, delayed pain over gore detail.

## 4. WEAPONS / DANGEROUS ACTS
Flag actionable step-by-step guidance for:
- making/modifying/acquiring/selling weapons;
- assassination/kidnapping/trafficking/sabotage;
- evading law enforcement/security;
- hiding evidence;
- drug production/trafficking/concealment;
- other dangerous illegal acts.

High-level fictional/tactical logic may remain when needed for causality but should not function as a real-world manual.

## 5. ROMANCE / SEXUAL CONTENT
Must flag:
- sexual violence;
- coercive sexual behavior framed as romance;
- explicit sexual acts/pornographic description;
- male-lead roughness/non-consensual touch toward heroine.

Romantic tension, affection and non-explicit consensual intimacy are acceptable within story contract.

## 6. HATE / TARGETED GROUPS
No slurs/hate framing.
Do not code real ethnic/religious/national/racial/protected groups as inherently criminal or inferior.
Villainy stays individual or fictional-organization based.

## 7. PROFANITY / HOOK
- avoid strong profanity as repeated style;
- keep hook particularly advertiser-aware;
- do not use shock wording only for click bait.

## 8. HOOK READINESS
First 30–90 seconds should:
- activate title promise;
- show status contrast/danger/anomaly;
- establish heroine stakes;
- open useful curiosity;
- avoid biography dump;
- avoid main-payoff spoiler;
- avoid gratuitous gore/profanity/sexual shock.

## 9. LONG-FORM RETENTION
For every Part:
- what changed in evidence, leverage, danger, relationship, status or understanding?
- can viewer leave without regret?
- are there repeated explanatory beats?
- does midpoint materially reshape story?
- does climax depend on established elements?
- does ending close title and emotional loops?

## 10. AUDIENCE VOICE
Default when user gives no demographic:
- mature American long-form storytelling;
- cinematic dark romance/thriller;
- clear, grounded, emotionally legible;
- no TikTok/meme slang unless character-specific;
- no faux-archaic “heritage” voice imported from another key;
- no purple prose every paragraph;
- suspense from consequence, leverage, evidence and relationship change.

## 11. FINAL PLATFORM GATE
Ready only when:
- no known YT-P0;
- no avoidable YT-P1 under normal monetization intent;
- fictional framing remains clear;
- violence non-graphic;
- crime detail non-instructional;
- romance consensual/non-explicit;
- no hate/group demonization;
- hook remains advertiser-aware.
