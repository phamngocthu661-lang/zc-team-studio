# LOGIC & CONTINUITY CHECKLIST — MAFIA MAID V1.1

## 0. CONTRACT
- Read `SHARED_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md`; raw DNA evidence cannot override operational continuity/consent/safety rules.

# 1. PART / OUTLINE GEOMETRY
- N Script Parts = N Outline Beats when outline available.
- 1:1 order preserved.
- Part performs its assigned Purpose/Key Events/Causal Chain/End Turn.
- No hidden extra Part inside headings or epilogue.

## 2. TIME / TRAVEL
- dates, clocks, day/night, elapsed time align;
- city-to-city/local travel is plausible;
- injuries, sleep, meetings, calls and recovery consume credible time;
- a character cannot appear in two places impossibly.

## 3. LOCATION / ACCESS / SECURITY
- room/floor/entrance/exit geometry remains consistent;
- access cards, guards, cameras, gates and safe rooms do not appear/disappear conveniently;
- a supposedly secured location needs a plausible breach mechanism;
- escape/rescue route uses established access;
- line of sight and cover match the described space.

## 4. KNOWLEDGE / POV
Character knowledge must come from:
- direct observation;
- credible communication;
- established records/system;
- established expertise + available evidence.

No narrator-to-character leakage.
Hypothesis stays hypothesis until verified.

## 5. OBJECT / EVIDENCE / RESOURCE CONTINUITY
Track:
- phones;
- keys/cards;
- documents/contracts/ledgers;
- vehicles;
- bags/clothing when plot-relevant;
- weapons/ammunition only at non-instructional story level;
- medical/technical tools;
- evidence originals/copies;
- money/transfers.

No teleport, duplicate original, spontaneous repair, unexplained possession or lost damage.

## 6. INJURY / PHYSICAL STATE
- wounds affect movement, grip, stamina and judgment;
- treatment/recovery is plausible;
- adrenaline may mask pain briefly, not erase injury;
- post-action crash is believable;
- serious injury cannot vanish next Part without time/treatment.

## 7. ACTION CAUSALITY
For major action:
trigger → decision → movement → opposition → complication → outcome → consequence.

Reject effortless win, conveniently incompetent enemy, infinite ammunition/stamina, impossible aim, or rescue with no setup.

## 8. HEROINE COMPETENCE
Every decisive competence beat should derive from:
training/experience + observed anomaly + check/test + inference + action.

No surprise genius in unrelated fields unless seeded.
No narrator label like “she was brilliant” substituting for process.

## 9. DUAL-PRESSURE SEQUENCE
If required by current writing contract:
- one immediate physical/safety/medical pressure;
- one simultaneous technical/intellectual problem;
- heroine manages both through already-seeded competence;
- scene remains physically coherent;
- it occurs inside an existing outline Beat.

## 10. RELATIONSHIP / CONSENT / POWER
- trust changes because of events;
- attraction does not erase fear or boundaries instantly;
- no coercive sexual behavior;
- no male-lead violence/roughness at heroine;
- protection evolves toward respect;
- marriage/coronation level matches title/outline.

## 11. ANTAGONIST INCENTIVES
- clear objective;
- comprehensible motive;
- available leverage;
- access to information/resources explained;
- escalation is rational from their perspective;
- defeat stems from protagonist action/evidence/choice, not sudden stupidity.

## 12. PROFESSIONAL AUTHORITY
- job/credential/experience fit acts performed;
- regulated medical/legal/security acts are not casually universalized;
- expert characters can seek specialist support;
- professional terminology stays consistent.

## 13. MONEY / CONTRACTS / BUSINESS
When used:
- amounts stay consistent;
- debt/salary/payment behavior is plausible;
- contract terms have consequences rather than functioning as magic paper;
- financial transfers/ownership/authority are not instant without story mechanism.

## 14. EVIDENCE STANDARD
High-stakes accusation should not depend on one convenient clue when the plot itself promises proof.
Prefer layered support:
physical/digital/document evidence + independent corroboration + competent interpretation.

## 15. SETUP / PAYOFF / OPEN LOOPS
For every setup:
- payoff;
- intentional unresolved status;
- or removal if obsolete.

For every payoff, setup must exist and be remembered accurately.

## 16. TITLE ANCHORS
If title promises Maid/Waitress/Baby/Clause/Poison/$X/Wedding/quote/etc., that element must be plot-relevant, not decorative.

## 17. MICRO-CONTINUITY
Check:
- names/spelling;
- forms of address;
- injuries;
- clothing/bag/key possession;
- weather/light;
- vehicle/location;
- doors/windows/security state;
- phone battery/damage;
- quoted document wording;
- relationship labels;
- threat status.
