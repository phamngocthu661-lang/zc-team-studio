# HUMAN PROSE & TTS NARRATIVE CHECKLIST — MAFIA MAID V1.1

## A. HUMAN FEEL WITHOUT ERRORS
Do not add mistakes to look human.
Human feel comes from specificity, asymmetry, motive, subtext, restraint, physical behavior and character-specific voice.

## B. AI-SIGNATURE AUDIT
Flag actionable occurrences of:
- repetitive one-line paragraphs;
- excessive fragments;
- repeated `Not X. Not Y. Z.`;
- repeated `For the first time...`;
- generic cinematic slogans;
- narrator explaining emotion after action already showed it;
- every scene ending in an aphorism;
- every character equally witty/polished;
- too-perfect comeback during danger;
- repeated metaphor/motif;
- exposition disguised as dialogue;
- repeated syntactic openings;
- artificial symmetry;
- adjective/adverb redundancy;
- melodramatic dark-romance clichés not earned by scene;
- overuse of em dashes as tension engine.

## C. CHARACTER VOICE
### Boss
- concise/directive tendency;
- can be emotionally restrained without sounding like a chatbot;
- no constant possession slogans;
- no long danger monologues.

### Heroine
- frightened/uncertain voice may be fuller;
- inside expertise, shorter/clinical tendency;
- must still sound like the same person;
- competence is demonstrated, not merely announced.

### Antagonists/secondary cast
- distinct diction and motive;
- not every henchman is a generic sneering caricature.

## D. DIALOGUE NATURALNESS
Ask:
- would this person really say this now?
- is information spoken only for audience convenience?
- is every answer unnaturally complete?
- is subtext possible?
- does relationship stage justify intimacy/possessiveness?
- does danger compress speech naturally?

## E. TTS READABILITY
- contractions natural;
- punctuation speakable;
- paragraphs not absurdly long;
- no production notes/camera/editing directions in copy-ready TTS script;
- **zero bracketed stage/audio/performance cues** under the standard contract;
- any `[beat]`, `[silence]`, `[breath]`, `[SFX]`, `[music]` or similar cue is actionable unless the latest user explicitly requested production markup;
- Part headings remain clear;
- no hidden continuity notes appear in visible script.

## F. RETENTION SHARPNESS
Patch when a scene:
- repeats known information;
- explains a reveal twice;
- pauses plot for generic philosophy;
- repeats romantic tension without relationship-state change;
- repeats threat without new leverage;
- delays title promise;
- contains documents/technical exposition without changing hypothesis/action.

Quiet scenes are allowed when they earn attachment, vulnerability, trust, contrast or decision.

## G. FORBIDDEN TROPE VOCABULARY SWEEP
Unless locked by title/outline quote/name, flag exact script-body occurrences of:
- fairy tale
- prince charming
- destiny
- helpless
- savior
- magic
- compromise
- ordinary

## H. AMERICAN ENGLISH / COPYEDIT
Check:
- American spelling;
- third-person past-tense consistency unless source locks otherwise;
- typo/duplicate/missing words;
- dialogue punctuation;
- capitalization;
- pronoun antecedents;
- proper-name consistency;
- number/date/unit consistency.

## I. PURE PREFERENCE FILTER
Do not flag a strong natural line only because a different line is possible.
