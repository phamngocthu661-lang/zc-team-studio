# 3. DNA CỐT LÕI — HEART + SPINE

## A. HEART DNA — QUIET HUMANITY

Giữ các yếu tố mạnh của slow-living healing storytelling:

- protagonist nghèo, vô gia cư hoặc mất cảm giác thuộc về;
- abandoned property có lịch sử;
- sensory realism;
- traditional craft / practical restoration;
- competence được thể hiện bằng hành động;
- small acts of kindness;
- family legacy;
- inheritance through work, not luxury;
- quiet dignity;
- emotional restraint;
- ending khiêm tốn, không Hollywood.

Core emotional progression thường là:

**Displacement → First Shelter → Careful Work → Human Connection → Responsibility → Belonging → Legacy**

## B. SPINE DNA — CINEMATIC MYSTERY RETENTION

Bắt buộc thiết kế:

- opening anomaly;
- primary mystery;
- deeper mystery;
- 8–12 useful clues;
- 4–7 reveal levels;
- at least 5 active open loops tổng thể;
- pressure escalation;
- staged discovery;
- title-level grand reveal;
- present-day consequence;
- emotional closure.

Retention progression lý tưởng:

**ANOMALY → QUESTION → CLUE → PARTIAL ANSWER → BIGGER QUESTION → PRESSURE → DISCOVERY → REINTERPRETATION → PERSONAL CONNECTION → HARD CHOICE → GRAND REVEAL → CONSEQUENCE → HEALING**

## EARLY RETENTION OVERRIDE — FIRST 5 MINUTES

Opening không được chỉ “gây tò mò rồi giữ bí mật”. Bắt buộc dùng:

**PROMISE CONTACT → PROOF → STAKES → MICRO-PAYOFF #1 → BIGGER QUESTION → CONTEXT-WHILE-MOVING → MICRO-PAYOFF #2**

Mandatory script-position handoff:
- title promise by ~200 chars;
- physical proof by ~400;
- stakes by ~650;
- micro-payoff #1 by ~1,100;
- bigger question + next action by ~2,000;
- micro-payoff #2 / functional clue by ~5,000.

**NO HOOK RESET:** không được hook bằng mystery rồi cắt sang biography dài. Backstory đầu video phải được nhúng vào current action; before first payoff, standalone biography is forbidden.

# 6A. ZERO-DEFECT CANON GATE — V2.3.1 HARD LOCK

Trước khi build 7-Part architecture, phải tạo nội bộ một **CANON STATE MODEL** theo `references/CANON_FORENSIC_HANDOFF.md`.

Các hạng mục sau là **HARD GATES**, không được bù bằng điểm tổng:

1. MASTER TIMELINE / season / year / elapsed-time consistency;
2. character ages và age-at-historical-event;
3. MONEY STATE arithmetic;
4. OBJECT LOCATION / PHYSICAL STATE;
5. WHO-KNOWS-WHAT;
6. ACCESS / AUTHORITY / LEGAL ROLE;
7. PROPERTY / PLACE NAMING INTRODUCTION;
8. COLD-OPEN EVENT identity + flashback return point;
9. EXACT-NUMBER classification: CHEKHOV NUMBER vs STATE NUMBER;
10. Part 6–7 RESOLUTION MODE;
11. title promise literal fulfillment;
12. why-not-found-earlier / why-now discovery causality;
13. full-script handoff supports **70,000–80,000 characters inclusive**;
14. First-5-Minute Early Retention Survival Map passes shared gates.

Nếu bất kỳ hard gate nào FAIL, outline **không được output**, kể cả tổng score >=98.

**STATE NUMBERS MUST BE RECOMPUTED, NOT RECALLED.**
Ví dụ tiền phải chạy `before → transaction → after`; không được chỉ ghi một con số canonical rồi nhớ lại bằng prose.

**NAME INTRODUCTION LOCK:** narrator/script downstream không được dùng tên property/room/mechanism mới trước naming beat hoặc documentary naming source đã được xác định trong outline.

**ONE DISCOVERY = ONE DISCOVERY:** nếu cold open là cùng một event với discovery sau flashback, outline phải gán cùng EVENT ID và chỉ dramatize một lần.

# 7. TITLE-PROMISE LOCK

Title là hợp đồng với viewer.

Phân tích:

## TITLE PROMISE OBJECT
Vật thể / địa điểm / căn phòng / bí mật viewer được hứa sẽ thấy.

## TITLE PROMISE QUESTION
Câu hỏi khiến viewer click.

## TITLE PROMISE EMOTION
Viewer kỳ vọng cảm giác gì: wonder, justice, inheritance, belonging, grief, hidden truth.

## TITLE PROMISE PAYOFF
Cảnh hoặc reveal nào phải hoàn thành lời hứa.

Rule:

- Không để noun/action chính trong title chỉ làm decoration.
- Không reveal toàn bộ payoff ngay opening.
- Grand reveal phải đủ lớn để justify click.
- Payoff phải xảy ra trước phần emotional aftermath, không để tới câu cuối mới reveal.

Tạo:

- homeless;
- living in car;
- recently evicted;
- job loss;
- inheritance dispute;
- temporary lodging;

Ví dụ:

Bắt buộc:
**Protagonist phải có agency.**

# 13. REVEAL LADDER

Thiết kế 4–7 tầng reveal.

## R1 — CONFIRMATION
Anomaly là thật.

## R2 — INTENTIONAL DESIGN
Ai đó cố tình ẩn / bảo vệ / thay đổi cấu trúc.

## R3 — HUMAN STORY
Con người đứng sau secret.

## R4 — PERSONAL CONNECTION
Connection với protagonist được seed từ trước, không coincidence vô lý.

## R5 — GRAND DISCOVERY
Title-level physical payoff.

## R6 — TRUE MEANING
Kho báu thật không chỉ là money/object.

## R7 — PRESENT CONSEQUENCE
Sự thật còn ảnh hưởng hiện tại.

Rule:

Major reveal không nên chỉ là “có thêm một chiếc hộp”.

Reveal tốt phải:
- reinterpret earlier clue;
- expand scale;
- personalize mystery;
- increase present stakes;
- force choice;
- change understanding of property.

# 14. PRESSURE ENGINE

Long-form cần resistance.

Chọn một hoặc kết hợp:

A. Developer / Buyer
B. Family Pressure
C. Legal / Institutional Pressure
D. Demolition Deadline
E. Structural / Weather Deadline
F. Financial Pressure
G. Ownership dispute
H. Historical preservation conflict

Nếu có HUMAN ANTAGONIST:

- motive phải rational;
- tránh cartoon villain;
- ưu tiên polite, controlled, financially logical hoặc legally cautious;
- antagonist ĐƯỢC PHÉP quay lại nhiều lần.

Thiết kế escalation:

### P0 — Early Stakes Clock / Pressure Shadow
Deadline, structural/weather risk, money clock, notice, rumor, schedule or outside interest may appear by ~15–25% when causally appropriate.

### P1 — Reasonable Pressure
Offer / warning / practical objection. Direct antagonist/institutional pressure usually becomes active around ~35–45% when the story uses one.

### P2 — Increased Leverage
Larger offer / deadline / complication.

### P3 — Knowledge Leak
Antagonist biết thứ họ lẽ ra chưa được biết hoặc phản ứng làm lộ motive.

### P4 — Final Pressure
Legal, financial, ownership, demolition or public consequence.

Mỗi lần quay lại phải tạo:
- NEW INFORMATION;
- NEW TEMPTATION;
- NEW STAKES;
hoặc
- NEW CONSEQUENCE.

Không lặp cùng một cuộc nói chuyện với số tiền cao hơn mà không đổi understanding.

# 15. DIFFICULT CHOICE RULE

Protagonist phải có ít nhất một lựa chọn mà cả hai phía đều hợp lý.

Ví dụ:

- sell vs continue investigation;
- use cache vs preserve it;
- demolish unsafe section vs stabilize original;
- reveal documents vs protect provenance;
- accept secure housing vs remain with difficult restoration.

Choice đúng phải có cost.

Nếu không mất gì, tension yếu.

Mỗi scene có:

PRACTICAL OBJECTIVE:
2–5 TECHNICAL DETAILS:
OBSTACLE:
PHYSICAL ACTION:
HUMAN BEAT:
CLUE DISCOVERED OR ADVANCED:

Technical detail có thể là:

- dimensions;
- tools;
- material;
- ratio;
- temperature;
- grit;
- price;
- cure time;
- historical construction method;
- repair sequence.

Rule:

**WORK MUST CREATE STORY.**

Ít nhất 2 clues phải được phát hiện nhờ protagonist làm việc cẩn thận.

Restoration và mystery không được chạy như hai cốt truyện riêng.

Ví dụ:

removing swollen baseboard → hidden alignment opening;
cleaning soot → maker's mark;
measuring exterior/interior → missing space;
repairing window → concealed cavity;
opening warped drawer → false back.

# 18. FAMILY-ANTAGONIST PAYOFF RULE

Nếu Part 1 nhấn mạnh một family antagonist hoặc người đã đẩy protagonist vào homeless state, không được quên loop này vô tình.

Chọn rõ:

OPTION A — Không quay lại, nhưng ending chứng minh protagonist không còn cần họ.

OPTION B — Quay lại một lần vì discovery thay đổi hoàn cảnh.

OPTION C — Hành vi cũ liên quan trực tiếp mystery.

OPTION D — Không xuất hiện trực tiếp nhưng payoff qua deed, letter, object, legal record hoặc final choice.

Không build antagonist mạnh rồi biến mất không payoff.

# 19. LETTER / DOCUMENT RULE

Letter không được giải thích toàn bộ mystery trong một lần.

Một letter tốt:

- có writer riêng;
- có factual detail;
- reveal character;
- answer một question;
- deepen another question;
- có practical detail nếu phù hợp.

Chia revelation giữa:

- objects;
- letters;
- maps;
- records;
- mechanisms;
- restoration;
- dialogue;
- external behavior.

# 21. 7-PART STORY ARCHITECTURE

Outline phải hỗ trợ finished script **70.000–80.000 ký tự inclusive**. Preferred operating zone là **74.000–78.000**. Đây là HARD total contract; outline nào không đủ material để đạt 70k mà không filler thì chưa READY.

Suggested planning allocation (soft, total range hard):

PART 1 — 10.200–11.000 chars
PART 2 — 10.200–11.000
PART 3 — 10.200–11.000
PART 4 — 10.200–11.000
PART 5 — 10.200–11.000
PART 6 — 11.800–12.800
PART 7 — 9.800–10.800

Có thể redistribute theo story, nhưng total script downstream **bắt buộc 70k–80k**. Mọi additional length phải đến từ causality, process, verification, pressure, human payoff, reaction/subtext, consequence hoặc clue/reveal work — không padding.

---

## PART 1 — PROMISE + PROOF + COMMITMENT

Purpose:
TITLE-PROMISE CONTACT → PHYSICAL PROOF → STAKES → MICRO-PAYOFF #1 → BIGGER QUESTION → CONTEXT-WHILE-MOVING → COMPETENCE → PROPERTY/ACCESS CONTEXT → PRIMARY ANOMALY DEEPENS.

Required:

- title noun/action/anomaly concretely present by first ~200 downstream script chars;
- physical/verifiable proof by ~400;
- why-it-matters-now stakes by ~650;
- first small answer/proof payoff by ~1,100;
- bigger question + protagonist next action by ~2,000;
- second functional clue/payoff by ~5,000;
- hardship and competence are woven into active action, not used as a long reset;
- no generic atmospheric preamble;
- no standalone biography before Micro-Payoff #1;
- if flashback is necessary, it is short and returns quickly; default is context-while-moving;
- by Part end, anomaly is deeper/more intentional than at opening.

Do not spend long on childhood or eviction chronology before the viewer receives proof/progress.

END:
A concrete changed state confirms the mystery is larger than the opening fact.

---

## PART 2 — INVESTIGATION + FIRST DEEPER PAYOFF

Purpose:
INVESTIGATION → COMMUNITY → COMPETENCE → FIRST DEEPER CLUE/PAYOFF → PRESSURE SHADOW.

Required:

- practical work;
- local/community character;
- measurement / repair / documentation;
- first historical or family clue;
- pressure is seeded/foreshadowed when causally appropriate; do not force a full confrontation this early;
- direct antagonist/institutional pressure should usually become active around 35–45% downstream, matching Script V1.6+;
- anomaly được xác nhận deliberate.

END:
Object / symbol / document / mechanism deepens mystery.

---

## PART 3 — FIRST OPENING

Purpose:
FIRST MECHANISM → HIDDEN SPACE → SENSORY DISCOVERY → HUMAN STORY → BIGGER QUESTION.

Required:

- skill solves first puzzle;
- slow physical opening;
- untouched evidence if relevant;
- human traces;
- first major partial payoff;
- pressure seed/foreshadow escalates toward the active window; do not force a full confrontation before causality/timing supports it;
- answer M1 but open M2/M3.

END:
A door, document, name, code or implication pointing deeper.

---

## PART 4 — HUMAN TRUTH

Purpose:
DEEPER HISTORY → PERSONAL CONNECTION → EMOTIONAL CENTER → MORAL WEIGHT.

Required:

- letters/records/objects distributed, not dump;
- historical/family truth;
- protagonist connection seeded and clarified;
- quiet kindness continues;
- discovery becomes more important than money;
- reveal creates present consequence.

END:
Unresolved final object/door/record/mechanism.

---

## PART 5 — PRESSURE + HARD CHOICE

Purpose:
EXTERNAL PRESSURE → TEMPTATION → FINAL CLUES → CHOICE → FINAL MECHANISM PREP.

Required:

- strongest believable pressure;
- real temptation;
- clue chain converges;
- antagonist motive becomes clearer;
- protagonist chooses with cost;
- final access logic prepared.

END:
At or immediately before title-level threshold.

---

## PART 6 — GRAND REVEAL + IMMEDIATE CONSEQUENCE

Purpose:
TITLE PAYOFF → STAGED REVEAL → TRUE MEANING → PRESENT CONSEQUENCE → **IMMEDIATE LEVERAGE CHANGE / PROVISIONAL OR FINAL PRESSURE OUTCOME ACCORDING TO RESOLUTION MODE**.

Không ép mọi legal / institutional / historical dispute phải kết thúc hoàn toàn trong Part 6. Nếu quy trình hợp lý cần nhiều tháng, Part 6 chỉ cần đạt temporary standstill, changed leverage, injunction avoided, preservation hold, negotiation opening hoặc equivalent provisional consequence.

Grand reveal pacing:

1. THRESHOLD
2. AIR / LIGHT / SMELL
3. FIRST OBJECT
4. EVIDENCE OF UNTOUCHED STATE
5. HUMAN DETAIL
6. DOCUMENT / RECORD
7. LARGER COLLECTION
8. TRUE PURPOSE
9. PRESENT CONSEQUENCE

Resolve external conflict through:

- evidence;
- law;
- preservation;
- provenance;
- ownership;
- public record;
- protagonist skill/decision.

Nếu `RESOLUTION MODE = PROCEDURAL`, chỉ resolve **immediate danger / leverage** ở Part 6; longer-term legal/ownership resolution chuyển sang Part 7 với time markers rõ.

Không dùng shouting match làm climax chính.

---

## PART 7 — LONGER-TERM RESOLUTION + HEALING + LEGACY

Purpose:
LONGER-TERM RESOLUTION IF NEEDED → AFTERMATH → SELECTIVE RESTORATION → COMMUNITY → HOME → LEGACY → CALLBACK.

Nếu `RESOLUTION MODE = PROCEDURAL`, Part 7 phải tiếp tục chronology một chiều qua winter/spring/months-later để hoàn tất settlement/review/verification trước khi đi vào healing. Không được Part 6 đi qua winter/spring rồi Part 7 quay ngược thời gian mà không có flashback marker.

Required:

- practical consequences;
- do not make property brand-new;
- resolve starting wound through physical action;
- callbacks to established object;
- modest future;
- optional time jump;
- no second giant climax.

Post-conflict material thường chỉ khoảng 10–15% story.

Mỗi epilogue scene phải làm ít nhất một:

- emotional payoff;
- symbolic callback;
- legacy;
- practical consequence;
- transformation.

# 21A. RESOLUTION MODE LOCK

Trước khi viết 7 Part, chọn đúng **ONE** mode và ghi vào Canon Handoff:

### MODE A — IMMEDIATE / PRIVATE RESOLUTION
Dùng khi hậu quả có thể hợp lý kết thúc gần grand reveal: private ownership clarification, family dispute, buyer withdrawal, simple contract choice, preservation hold already authorized.

Part 6 có thể hoàn tất external conflict; Part 7 là aftermath/healing.

Dùng khi có:
- court process;
- historical water/property rights;
- title chain;
- probate;
- government review;
- survey / archive verification;
- preservation designation;
- institutional investigation.

Part 6 chỉ cần: evidence threshold + immediate consequence + provisional standstill / changed leverage.
Part 7 tiếp tục chronology bằng explicit time markers để đi tới negotiated/provisional/final outcome hợp lý.

**Không ép MODE B thành instant victory chỉ để giữ cấu trúc cũ.**

# 23. RETENTION MAP — TWO-SCALE REQUIRED

## A. FIRST 5-MINUTE SURVIVAL MAP — HARD

Outline phải handoff explicit beats cho:
- ~0–200 chars: TITLE PROMISE CONTACT;
- by ~400: PHYSICAL PROOF;
- by ~650: STAKES;
- by ~1,100: MICRO-PAYOFF #1;
- by ~2,000: BIGGER QUESTION + NEXT ACTION;
- by ~5,000: MICRO-PAYOFF #2 / FUNCTIONAL CLUE.

No Hook Reset. Before first payoff, no standalone biography. In first ~2,000 chars, no continuous exposition/backstory block >~500 chars without returning to active action/evidence/stakes.

## B. MACRO RETENTION MAP

Default reference:

0–2% — title promise + proof + first micro-payoff
5–10% — anomaly deepens / intentionality or first deeper clue
12–18% — substantial partial answer / mechanism progress
20–30% — first hidden-space / historical-human reveal
20–30% — pressure shadow/clock when causally appropriate
35–45% — direct antagonist/institutional pressure active when applicable
45–55% — deeper mystery + personal connection / reinterpretation
55–68% — pressure, temptation, difficult choice
68–76% — final mechanism / clue convergence
78–85% — title-level grand discovery
85–92% — ultimate meaning + present consequence
92–100% — emotional/procedural closure

After the first ~5,000 chars, no planned stretch >~6,500 downstream script chars may contain only repair/travel/description/reflection without meaningful state change.

Meaningful change gồm:
- new information;
- stakes/consequence;
- decision;
- relationship change affecting action;
- clue/payoff;
- discovery;
- reinterpretation;
- pressure/leverage shift;
- functional mechanism/restoration progress.

# 26. HISTORICAL / REALISM RULE

Nếu story dùng historical material:

- không romanticize quyền lực;
- flawed figure không trở thành saint chỉ vì regret;

- old documents không magically transfer modern ownership;
- professional verification có thể được dùng;
- provenance phải matter nếu discovery lớn.

Technical detail chỉ dùng khi:

1. reveal competence;
2. create/resolve conflict;
3. affect causality.

Story + Character + Retention first.
