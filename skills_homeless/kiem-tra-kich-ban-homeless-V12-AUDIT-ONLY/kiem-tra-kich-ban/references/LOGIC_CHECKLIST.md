# LOGIC CHECKLIST V1.1 — EXHAUSTIVE

Use on both complete reads. Small inconsistencies count if they should actually be fixed.

## 1. PHYSICAL / MECHANICAL
- Weight, force, leverage, load path.
- Old mechanisms account for corrosion/settlement/wear.
- Heavy door/slab/safe/object moved plausibly.
- Tools fit task, material, access, and user's skill level.
- Original fragile artifact is not used for avoidable torque/force.
- Measurement and drawing scale are correct.
- Fasteners, hinges, locks, masonry, timber, floors and openings behave plausibly.
- Sequence of physical operations is possible in the shown space.

## 2. MATERIAL SURVIVAL
- Paper, ink, wax, leather, textile, wood, metal age realistically.
- “Untouched” = arrangement undisturbed, not magically new.
- Good preservation has environmental explanation.
- Moisture, insects, animals, UV, temperature, fire and theft risks are addressed when relevant.

## 3. SPACE / ARCHITECTURE
- Interior/exterior dimensions reconcile.
- Hidden volume has credible concealment.
- Entrances, stairs, floors, roof, windows, chimneys obey geometry.
- A secret large enough to notice needs layered concealment logic.
- Characters can physically see/reach what narration says they see/reach.
- Routes/access restrictions remain consistent.

## 4. TIME / TRAVEL
- Dates and ages line up everywhere.
- Deadlines allow all actions shown.
- Travel time/weather/daylight are plausible.
- Repairs/research/legal processes take credible time.
- Meals, nights, working hours, phone calls and institutional response times do not silently compress implausibly.

## 5. KNOWLEDGE / POV
- Character cannot know unseen/offscreen/private facts without source.
- Hypothesis remains hypothesis until verified.
- Narrator knowledge does not leak into dialogue.
- Character remembers/forgets facts consistently.
- Later confidence level matches prior evidence.

## 6. DISCOVERY CAUSALITY
Every major discovery should derive from:
access + competence + anomaly + test + evidence.

Avoid:
random drawer → perfect clue;
dog digs exact treasure without setup;
wrong turn → final secret;
letter conveniently explains everything;
clue appears only because plot now needs it.

For each discovery ask why this action produces this evidence now.

## 7. WHY-NOT-FOUND-EARLIER
Use enough credible factors:
- false feature;
- inaccessible area;
- wrong authoritative record copied later;
- structural danger;
- fragmented archives;
- later repair obscures original feature;
- no reason to inspect until new anomaly appears.

Large/old secrets normally require stacked reasons, not one excuse.

## 8. LAW / PROPERTY / INHERITANCE
- Deed/will/ledger/letter = evidence, not instant legal outcome.
- Authentication + corroboration + interpretation precede major consequence.
- Title chain, easement, taxes, claimant, jurisdiction issues acknowledged as needed.
- Protagonist should not speak as lawyer unless qualified.
- Interim actions (freeze/suspension/injunction/review) precede final outcomes where realistic.
- Legal labels remain consistent across scenes.

## 9. MONEY / ECONOMIC BEHAVIOR
- Sale price, auction, salary, repairs, debt, housing, transport, taxes have internal plausibility.
- Desperate character weighs material security realistically.
- Large organizations have budgets and due diligence.
- Exact money amounts stay consistent.
- Offers/contracts have credible conditions, guarantees, and incentives.

## 10. PROFESSIONAL AUTHORITY
- Age/credential/experience fit role.
- Engineer/conservator/lawyer/doctor/police involved when truly required.
- Hero can be competent without magically holding every license.
- Job title, reporting line and promotion pace are credible.
- Characters do not perform regulated/specialist acts beyond believable authority without support.

## 11. INSTITUTIONAL COMPETENCE
- Antagonists do not ignore obvious basics.
- If institution makes mistake, explain structural reason: bad inherited data, conflict, cost, classification, missing evidence.
- Better antagonist failure = new evidence changes the problem.
- Officials respond proportionally rather than magically fast/slow for plot convenience.

## 12. HISTORICAL / ARCHIVAL
- Records have plausible creators/purpose.
- Administrative docs sound administrative, not screenplay exposition.
- Different archives can preserve independent copies.
- Historical letter should not use implausibly modern rhetoric without setting justification.
- Paper trail survives in a distributed, physically plausible way.
- Historical terminology and institutional roles do not contradict the story's own era.

## 13. OBJECT CONTINUITY
Track every important object:
- who has it;
- where it is;
- condition;
- whether it was opened/moved/damaged;
- whether copy/original/replica status changes;
- later callbacks.

No object teleport, spontaneous repair, duplicate original, or forgotten damage.

## 14. EXACT DETAIL / CHEKHOV
Inspect every exact:
- time;
- date/year;
- age;
- price/salary;
- measurement/distance;
- room number;
- quantity;
- serial/ID/odd label.

Precision creates expectation. Pay it off, use it to verify/limit something, keep it consistent, or remove unnecessary emphasis.

## 15. CHARACTER BEHAVIOR / INCENTIVE
- Decisions fit economic/emotional reality.
- Risk tolerance matches circumstances.
- Character does not reject valuable options simply to look morally pure.
- Antagonist motive is concrete and coherent.
- Reaction intensity matches what character actually knows at that moment.

## 16. SETUP / PAYOFF / CALLBACK
For every setup, track whether it:
- pays off;
- intentionally remains unresolved;
- becomes obsolete after rewrite.

For every payoff, verify a setup exists and is remembered accurately.

## 17. MICRO-CONTINUITY
Check small but fixable slips:
- handedness/position when relevant;
- doors/windows open vs closed;
- coat/bag/key/tool possession;
- weather changes;
- light/time-of-day;
- injuries/fatigue;
- room labels/names;
- relationship terms;
- spelling/name consistency;
- quoted document wording that changes later.


## EARLY RETENTION SURVIVAL — CRITICAL
- Promise <=~200 chars
- Proof <=~400
- Stakes <=~650
- Micro-Payoff #1 <=~1,100
- Bigger question + action <=~2,000
- Micro-Payoff #2 <=~5,000
- No Hook Reset
- No ~6,500-char retention desert after opening
