# STRICT TITLE SCORING RUBRIC

## HARD ELIGIBILITY GATES — CHECK BEFORE SCORING

A candidate must be **100 characters or fewer**, counting all visible characters including spaces and punctuation.

- <=100 characters: eligible for scoring.
- >100 characters: **LENGTH REJECTED**; do not assign a score and do not allow it to become a finalist.

The 98-100 score threshold applies only after the candidate passes all hard eligibility gates.

### Instant Comprehension
The core premise must be understandable on first pass by a general U.S. 40+ viewer. Obscure jargon that must be explained before curiosity works = REJECT unless performance data specifically supports it.

### Click-to-Opening Serviceability
The promise must be dramatizable immediately downstream: title contact by ~200 script chars and physical proof by ~400, without giving away the final answer. A title that inherently requires long backstory before its mystery makes sense = REJECT.

Use this reference when scoring candidates.

| Dimension | Max |
|---|---:|
| Viewer Contract Strength | 15 |
| CTR / Curiosity Gap | 15 |
| Viral DNA Preservation | 15 |
| Freshness / Originality | 10 |
| Keyword Strength | 10 |
| Emotional Trigger | 10 |

| Thumbnail Synergy | 7 |
| Native English & Rhythm | 5 |
| Logic & Story Potential | 5 |
| **TOTAL** | **100** |

## Interpretation
- 98-100: READY TO USE
- 95-97: Strong but NOT READY under this skill
- 90-94: Good base, requires another rewrite cycle
- 80-89: Major weaknesses
- Below 80: Rebuild substantially

## Important
Do not inflate scores. A title must actually satisfy the rubric to receive 98+.
