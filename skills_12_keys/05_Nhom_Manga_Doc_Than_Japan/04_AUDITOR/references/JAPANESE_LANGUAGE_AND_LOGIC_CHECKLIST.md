# JAPANESE VOICE COMIC QA CHECKLIST — JA-JP

## Narrative correctness
1. Same age at every milestone and elapsed year; no birth age resetting.
2. `独身`, `離婚`, `死別` not interchangeable; don't imply protagonist single when married unless title permits change.
3. If heroine is mother, specify child age, guardianship and boundaries; no child made merely a plot bargaining chip.
4. Respect workplace authority: HR can authorize hiring, line managers do not spontaneously grant corporate ownership.
5. Days/months and travel modes plausible; distance and timing not teleportation.
6. Hospital actions realistic; 119 for ambulance in Japan, no instant impossible recovery.
7. Physical keys, rent contract, unsigned agreements and money have traceable custody.
8. Story wouldn't collapse if two adults communicate normally; secrets require meaningful reason with payoff.
9. Female character can refuse, initiate, reconsider; romance grows from consent, trust and shared actions.
10. Climax resolves visible obstacle using earned skill and sacrifices; surprise status alone is insufficient.

## Japanese
1. Stable kanji spellings for every name in entire manuscript; avoid introducing same person with similar names.
2. Register: peers casual, workplace 敬語 where realistic; `俺` narration vs `私` in formal speech can switch intentionally but explain.
3. Do not mix `あなた`, `お前`, `君`, `〜さん`, `〜様`, `社長`, `課長` randomly.
4. Common particles `が/は/を/に/で/と/へ`, negatives, passive and honorific construction checked in context.
5. `話` vs `話し`, `会う` vs `遭う`, `意外` vs `以外`, `決める` vs `決まる` are context-dependent; no naive find-replace.
6. Dates and numeric expressions match across Parts (55歳 → 56歳 only if birthday and time elapsed).
7. Avoid nonnative calques and redundant explanations: action/subtext preferred.
8. Quotation punctuation `「」` and ellipsis; no nested unreadable bracket dialogue for TTS.
9. TTS tags should not be spoken; accidental `[serious]` in narration body and incomplete `@speaker:` labels are defects.
10. Check the end of each Part and start of next for duplicated or missing lines after staged assembly.

## Formatting machine checks don't catch
Automated scripts detect missing headings, length, role syntax and mechanical repetitions; they CANNOT truly certify correct Japanese usage, story logic, viewer enjoyment, or competitor superiority. Treat these as editorial reviews and record uncertainty.
