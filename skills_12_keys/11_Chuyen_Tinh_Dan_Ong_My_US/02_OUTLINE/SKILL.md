# STEP 2 — ORIGINAL ROMANCE OUTLINE / LOGIC PREFLIGHT
Read `../00_SHARED/SHARED_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md`, `../00_SHARED/CHANNEL_DNA.md`, `references/OUTLINE_ENGINE.md`.

Input `CURRENT FINAL TITLE`, `LOCKED_TITLE`, `PRIMARY_DNA`, `STEP2_HANDOFF`. No title substitution. One story only, no script. Build two full adult characters with independent desires, visible flaws, actual work/family obligations, boundaries and a believable reason to meet. Specify states and calendars (day/month/season), travel time, who knows what and when, money/authority constraints, active relationship status. Man has arc beyond competence; woman's independent objective matters to final choice.

Plan 7 parts and 22–28 causal scenes. Every scene includes `SCENE_ID`, when/where, scene's concrete event, why it follows, a distinctive romantic or emotional beat, and change in trust/stakes. Include at least 3 visually memorable specific situations, differentiated exchanges, gradual closeness and at least one inconvenient genuine choice for each lead. Make midpoint change their possibilities, late setback credible, repair behavior measurable, outcome neither forced nor merely a sermon. Mark setups/payoffs. No repetitive lectures about boundaries or responsibility. Outline 8–12 continuity risks and how writer can address them; use plausible US logistics, not invented exact addresses. All romance participants adult and free to choose.

Create explicit per-part SOFT word ranges summing preferably 7,300–7,700, within hard total 6,700–8,100 unless override; mark budgets as `SOFT` and include 100-word upper-limit reserve. Evaluate 100 editorial: logic 35 / chemistry 20 / retention 20 / character 15 / US realism 10. `OUTLINE_READY` requires >=95 (subjective editorial) AND no unresolved causal impossibility. Deliver `STEP2_outline.md`, `OUTLINE_ID`, 7-part 22–28 scene map, continuity sheet, `STEP3_HANDOFF` with all fixed entities and events.
