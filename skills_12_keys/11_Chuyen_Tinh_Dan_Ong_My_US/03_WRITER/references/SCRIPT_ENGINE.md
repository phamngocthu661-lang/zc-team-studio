# SCRIPT ENGINE V1.1 — WRITING INSTRUCTIONS
- Read locked scene map before composing. Every on-page scene should have causal motive and at least one changing beat. Avoid filler monologues just to add words.
- Hook: offer + dilemma + subtle intimacy within ~80–150 words; first person US idiom and instantly visible action.
- Character voices: man has flawed default behavior and specific everyday competence; woman makes independent choices, teases or disagrees intelligently; both change under pressure. At least three scenes where her action genuinely surprises him without violating character logic.
- Romance beats: observed detail > mutual interpretation > choice > cost > later payoff. Distinguish emotional vulnerability from exposition. Do not write multiple consecutive pages of counseling-like speeches, slogans, or repetitive 'I was learning...' conclusions.
- Conflict: distinguish real unavoidable duty from avoidable selfish default. Show safety/work consequences responsibly; never sacrifice public safety for date-night gesture.
- Timeline: keep day/week/month in light-touch references, times reasonable for US driving, re-check flight/transport and local organizations only when plot needs them. Do not invent laws/insurance requirements with false certainty.
- Structural delivery: save seven clean draft part bodies; use `\n\n` between parts in clean TTS. Once user approves run QA, downstream part files must be regenerated from FINAL (not draft). Don't claim exact counts until real file counted.
- Closing: echo one concrete motif (seat, object, recurring exchange) but not as a moral paragraph; finish with chosen ongoing behavior, avoid 'and that was when I realized love...'.
