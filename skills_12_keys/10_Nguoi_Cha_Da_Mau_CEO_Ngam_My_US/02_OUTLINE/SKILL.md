# SKILL 2 — OUTLINE ARCHITECT V2 (S2)

Read `references/SHARED_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md`, `references/STORY_DNA.md`, `references/HOOK_RETENTION_SPEC.md` and S1 manifest. Lock TITLE verbatim. Write English story decisions suitable for adult US audience; operational handoff can include Vietnamese. No finished script at S2.

## Three gates before outlining
1. **Literal title truth**: Who *actually* shut down/fired/saved/owned what? Distinguish cause from legal authority. Never pretend a corporate donor owns an independent nonprofit. If title locked and conflict unrepairable, STOP and request a new authorized title/version.
2. **Hook arithmetic**: 150 WPM default => 75/150/225 words at 30/60/90 seconds. Specify exact COST, CLUE, QUESTION events and word-budget cap for each; outline-specific early event must be budgeted <=225 words if contract says <=90s. Do not promise a receipt reveal at 60–90s and schedule it at minute four.
3. **Mystery reveal control**: Distinguish audience knowledge from hero knowledge, secret identity vs transparent dramatic irony. Never blurt the secret via narrator in first 90 sec when the suspense depends on audience guessing; if ironic early reveal is purposeful, explicitly write a reason and replacement suspense goal.

## Output `02_outline.md` plus frozen `02_hook_contract.json`
A) `LOCKED_TITLE`, `VIDEO_ID`, DNA family, creative uniqueness vs last topics, audience, POV and setting.
B) Cast bible: father competence, dependent child age/school/custody/transport, CEO identity/reveal clock, antagonist rational stakes, witnesses, realistic medical/technical/legal limits.
C) Timeline bible: exact weekdays and dates + hour windows, childcare, paid shift, object custody, money/property/legal ownership, who knows what when, verification timeline; use Python for weekday/date if available.
D) Seven PARTs, 5–9 meaningful beats EACH, word budgets totaling 6,500–8,000 default; every beat `cause → choice → new cost or evidence → downstream consequence` and a unique turn, not repetitive discussion.
E) 0–30 / 30–60 / 60–90 sec beat-by-beat **<=225 word** opening plan at 150 WPM; three mandatory proof events COST/CLUE/QUESTION with target word_start; any additional user-locked early event included, and what is deliberately not revealed.
F) Evidence ledger with source, visibility, independent corroboration and limitation; state what proof does and does not establish. Logical title-payoff setup and exact opening-event vs eventual evidence showdown distinction.
G) Retention: distinctive new stake/change roughly every 4–7 mins, midpoint hypothesis reversal, deepest moral cost in P5, proof+repair in P6, visible family change in P7. Anchor acts to emotional decisions and imperfect human consequences, not repetitive ledger summaries.
H) Risk register and audience emotion curve; S2 editorial score: logic 30, agency 20, retention 15, evidence 15, emotion 10, plausibility 10; target >=95 with ZERO hard faults. Self-scoring is a planning signal, not QA proof.
