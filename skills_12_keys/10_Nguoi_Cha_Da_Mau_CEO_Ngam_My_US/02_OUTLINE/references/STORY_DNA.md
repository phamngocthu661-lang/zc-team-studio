# STORY DNA V2 — five modular US families

A — UNDERCOVER OWNER × JUSTICE: low-status encounter, concealed identity retained for purpose, unfair act, verifiable evidence, human consequences, bounded executive authority. Undercover CEO reveal can be middle act; no accidental omniscient spoiler.
B — KINDNESS × RESPONSIBLE SINGLE FATHER: father's generous choice costs something while he still protects his child; return benefit is an earned practical opportunity, not sudden lottery money. Clear distinction between genuine beneficiary and business owner.
C — DISMISSED EXPERT × MEASURABLE PROOF: mechanic, school cook, driver or technician misjudged; diagnosis or skill demonstrated, independent check and safety sign-off. No instant aircraft miracle repair.
D — FAMILY PROTECTION × SERVICE BACKGROUND: veteran or first responder credentials only if established; father uses calm observation and ordinary procedures. Daughter/son has original motivation and not merely kidnapped/injured to motivate plot.
E — FEMALE CEO × SINGLE DAD SECOND CHANCE: class/power misunderstanding, father boundaries, CEO accountability, mutual respect, optional slow romance requiring conflict-of-interest resolution. Do not equate romance with repayment.

**Selection**: select one primary and at most one secondary. Never force all five families into one story; a CEO can remain employer, ally, or antagonist. When referencing high-view competitor concepts, abstract the emotional mechanism and innovate with new setting, evidence, skill, and family dilemma. All major turns must be caused by character choices; each setup must pay off physically or relationally.
