# QA LOGIC / SPELLING / PRESERVATION V2

1. **Calendar and care:** dates vs weekdays, time-of-day, school hours, childcare pickup (include commute), body knows information only after learning it, age and relationship.
2. **Actual authority:** CEO can stop own funding, not order unrelated nonprofit board to lock a door. Ownership registry, firing process, inspections, medicine, law enforcement, aircraft/vehicle repair only if realistic, testable and timed.
3. **Causal proof:** clue source → receipt/authorization → independent verification → opposing account → consequence; no original signature alone equals guilty verdict; no unearned miraculous rescue.
4. **Title truth:** if "shut down" applies only to one missed lunch while other services continue, discuss proportionality and exact causal chain. A title lock does not authorize a lie.
5. **Secret identity:** for every character and viewer, what was known by word 225? Flashbacks may change time but must not break the payoff logic. Narrator claiming CEO surprise while already exposing identity at word 220 is inconsistent.
6. **Hook math:** at default 150 WPM max 225 words by 90 sec. Compute actual position of explicit outline early events, COST/CLUE/QUESTION. Check final anchors after authorized repair, not approximate by paragraph count.
7. **en-US proofread:** spellings, punctuation, article/preposition use, pronoun agreement, dialogue tags, repetitions, character names, tense/POV. Avoid flagging legitimate colloquial dialogue as error.
8. **One-shot revision:** severity CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW; `exact_before`, one unique match, `exact_after`, dependency-closed impacted spans. If not fixable by one scoped patch, stop for human. Final QA cannot edit text or ask writer to do another automatic pass.
9. **Maturity and pacing:** tangible parental sacrifice, diverse human scenes, antagonistic dilemma, midpoint new knowledge, non-repetitive documentation, completed emotional story and consequences.

Evidence table: `defect_id | severity | start_word | exact_quote | why_wrong | impact | authorized_span | corrected_behavior`. Make reader-relevant critique specific; no claim to have done a 'triple read' if not independently verifiable.
