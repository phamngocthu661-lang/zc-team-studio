# CANON AND HANDOFF REQUIREMENTS

Format title lock, video_id, time-stamped facts. Example canonical records (fictional demo, not mandatory content):

- `CHAR|Rogelio Ávila|71|father|walks with cane after knee surgery; no unexplained recovery`
- `CHAR|Arturo Ávila|39|son|fears default on restaurant lease; believes father will guarantee it`
- `ASSET|workshop|owner Rogelio documented in escritura|not Arturo's; transfer never signed`
- `MONEY|pension monthly MXN 11,000|spending MXN 8,000|any money transfer changes balance`
- `EVIDENCE|lease draft|signed by whom and when|only becomes available to Rogelio after Part4`
- `WHY_NOW|request to sign sale before contract deadline|deadline source documentary evidence`
- `RULE|Rogelio may refuse his own guarantee; cannot freeze Arturo's personal bank account`

Before output, self-check all dates/ages/ownership and every recurring prop. Each Part must change credible power balance and emotional stake. Avoid assuming all older fathers in Mexico have large pensions or own luxury villas; introduce class/region intentionally. Separation between scene facts, reveal timing, narrator interpretation, and actual legal truth is mandatory.
