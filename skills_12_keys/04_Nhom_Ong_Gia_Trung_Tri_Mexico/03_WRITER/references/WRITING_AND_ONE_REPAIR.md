# HOW TO PRESERVE VOICE AND DO EXACTLY ONE REPAIR

Drafting A/B/C is production, NOT repair. QA baseline is a separate independent pass. One authorized repair is a single splice operation touching any number of preapproved, nonoverlapping zones; they are all delivered and applied at once. QA then performs one independent postrepair evaluation but cannot return to writer for a second correction.

When rewriting a zone: quote original, explain classification and defect, show logical consequence for neighboring unchanged paragraphs, preserve strong emotional lines outside the zone. Do not alter any spans outside approved byte windows, even whitespace. If a typo is in a protected strong quote, QA must choose not to touch it or create a new, separately approved VIDEO VERSION; don't bypass protection. If a correction would require modifying an entire Part or multiple Part headings, the project is not eligible for surgical repair.

The scripts verify SHA, allowed byte-window, protected anchors, UTF-8 boundaries and full 7-Part length. They cannot validate Mexican legal facts or subjective storytelling quality; QA/editor must do that.

Step5 `--user-approved` is caller claim that the user requested this one revision. The tool cannot validate account identity. Never invoke without explicit Step5 authorization.
