# Audience retention and source fidelity
- 0–25 sec: open with a **micro-story** and audible consequence; one detail, one question (e.g. what 'cheap rent' omitted), not 1min channel introduction.
- 25–90 sec: introduce theme, promise 30 encounters and a better-informed real decision; place review #1 within opening.
- #2–10: escalate practical stakes, alternate tone, don't frontload 10 similar losses.
- #11–20: shift from external money trap to dignity and family/work change; include at least two cases where problem was solved.
- #21–27: strongest newly learned lesson, no duplicated hook.
- #28–30: payoff in useful ambiguity, last one a concrete protective decision.
- No invented “source testimony”; original reconstructed voices. Character voices must vary in idiom, age, agency, status, location, reason for decision; Japanese polite vs plain distinctions should fit person.
- For each source competitor pattern used record `MECHANIC_LEARNED`, `NEW_SCENARIO`, `COPIED_TEXT=false`.
