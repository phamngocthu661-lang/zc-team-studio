# Mexican celebrity research canon, fact ledger, production rule

Use public interviews, contemporary press, archived broadcasts, official institutions, books and music/film production credits, attributed statements, reliable court records where public, memorial records for cause of death only if clearly documented. For posthumous portrayals, still require accuracy and context.

## Mandatory source row
`S01 | publisher / author | direct URL | published YYYY-MM-DD or unknown | accessed YYYY-MM-DD | source class | what it verifies | conflicts | reliability notes`

## Mandatory claim row
`C01 | claim (precise proposition) | FIRST_PUBLIC_DATE | EVENT_DATE | source SIDs | VERIFIED/ATTRIBUTED_CONTESTED/INFERENCE/UNSUPPORTED | material or sensitive? | approved narration text`.

Never pretend that a biography's strong rhetoric, an AI model summary, another YouTube description, or a Wikipedia reference alone establishes allegations. Wikipedia can be a discovery lead; follow source citations. If direct browsing unavailable, use only user-provided primary source bytes/links and flag `NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED`.

## Canon checks
- Date: event occurrence ≠ date public learned ≠ interview publication.
- Age and length of relationships: verify from records, avoid calculating from uncertain birthdays.
- Marriage/divorce/child claims: do not infer from surname, tabloids or photos.
- Death and health: cite known public cause only, avoid unsourced medical causation and "karma".
- Court matters: allegation ≠ lawsuit filed ≠ investigation ≠ verdict ≠ appeal outcome; include status as of research date.
- Quotes: copy exact short excerpt only if verified and attributable; otherwise paraphrase honestly without quotes.
- Rights: public-domain/public license or permission for archival images and songs; no assumption a channel sample grants reuse rights.
- No undisclosed recreations of purported proof documents/photos; in thumbnail call fictional composite "conceptual illustration" if not evidence.

## Narrative sophistication
Try to explain why a decision mattered at the time, what credible alternate account existed, what audience expected, and what reliably changed later. When source evidence disagrees, attribute each view and stop before declaring a definitive secret.
