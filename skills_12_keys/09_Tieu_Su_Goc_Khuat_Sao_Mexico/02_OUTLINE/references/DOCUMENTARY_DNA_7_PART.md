# SEVEN PART TRUE-LIFE DOCUMENTARY DNA: TRAS LA FAMA

## Emotional promise and source distinction
The powerful engine is not humiliation, but a credible **contrast**: a beloved public image / great artistic achievement versus a documented consequential choice or personal challenge and later ramifications. The narrator is investigative but evidence-led. Dignity and complexity matter even in scandal cases.

### Arc 1: image vs facts
P1 contrast event -> P2 career idolization -> P3 first public contradiction -> P4 primary documents/interviews and chronology -> P5 responses/counterevidence -> P6 consequence -> P7 reassess public myth.
### Arc 2: rupture / family and partnership
P1 verifiable breakup/public statement -> P2 collaborators and ascent -> P3 signs in public record -> P4 verifiable moment of change -> P5 more than one perspective -> P6 consequences -> P7 complexity and shared legacy.
### Arc 3: fall and return
P1 career turning point -> P2 road to success -> P3 pressure context -> P4 failure/retreat public events -> P5 support and disagreement -> P6 return or lasting transition -> P7 legacy without overclaiming diagnosis.
### Arc 4: turning point
P1 decision -> P2 origin and constraints -> P3 alternatives -> P4 exact choice in source -> P5 visible consequences -> P6 public impact -> P7 reinterpretation.
### Arc 5: legacy and unsolved questions
P1 genuine open question -> P2 iconic past -> P3 contemporary sources -> P4 evidence, not fake twist -> P5 contradictions and limits -> P6 confirmed later facts -> P7 acknowledge what remains unknown.

## Per-chapter functions
- Part 1/Cold Open: real public stakes first, context second, do not make a claim narrator cannot prove.
- Part 2: recognizable achievements and relationships; emotional investment; avoid lengthy chronological encyclopedia.
- Part 3: first consequential tension with dated evidence; not a "sinister secret" invented for retention.
- Part 4: strongest verified turning point, primary quote/document attribution, address alternative accounts.
- Part 5: distinguish consequences for public career vs allegations or contested interpretation.
- Part 6: show causal evidence for outcomes, not mystical karma, and do not treat health/disability as punishment.
- Part 7: documented outcome, human legacy, modest unanswered question and meaningful viewer CTA.

## Anti-cliches
No "nadie imaginaba", "todos callaron", "el destino lo castigó", "el mundo se paralizó" unless it is literally documented and meaningful. Never exploit bereavement or disability as cosmic retribution. No repeated summary cliffhangers, no five fake revelations about one recycled interview. True dramatic pacing comes from decisions, chronology, stakes and competing public narratives.
