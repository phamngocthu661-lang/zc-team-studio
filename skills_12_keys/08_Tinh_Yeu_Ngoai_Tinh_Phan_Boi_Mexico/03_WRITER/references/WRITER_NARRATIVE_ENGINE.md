# LONG-FORM MEXICAN TELENOVELA NARRATIVE ENGINE

## Emotional mechanisms inspired by supplied sample patterns
- Public betrayal: heroine is excluded at gala/event; climax reveals a traceable competence/contract; she does not rely on a powerful man to hand her status.
- Childbirth abandonment: compassion first, logistical focus on care network and informed consent; true wrong is betrayal/absence, not invented death sentence.
- Former love dilemma: husband chooses ex, heroine sees pattern of dismissed needs; later hard decision earned, not instant rebound.
- Corporate money: forged/hidden invoices, board roles, signatures, civil processes, accounting trail; proof is documented before payoff.
- Family betrayal: family members have plausible loyalties, not a monolithic mob; children have age-appropriate perspective.
- Quiet exit: "silence" means intentional boundaries and practical action, not 60 minutes of passive suffering.

## The 8–12 minute progression method
A new beat must (1) update what reader knows, (2) change a character's options, or (3) impose a fresh consequence. Don't add more shouting without change. Mix high-arousal scenes with quieter specific daily-life details. Each scene uses `want -> obstacle -> action -> consequence`; a scene that replays same anger without any delta should be shortened.

## POV continuity
In 1st person, the narrator may truthfully recount what she learned from an email, testimony or later recording, but may not narrate private thoughts of another character as confirmed fact. Mark flashbacks as clear temporal shifts and return to current line. Use names consistently; when quoting, keep apostrophe and accents proper for Mexican Spanish.

## Strong prose tests
- Sensory detail tied to decision (fabric stain vs wedding invite, receipt ink vs denial, cough of child vs absence) instead of generic luxury imagery.
- Dialogues change power or disclose character, not only "villain says humiliating thing" repeated.
- Antagonist's tactical move must draw from his resources, not omnipotence; heroine's plan must include constraints, allies with distinct stakes, imperfect information.
- End with visual action (leaving key, opening studio, picking child up, looking at one's own name on contract) rather than summary saying "aprendí a valorarme".

## Don't reproduce exact sample stories
References identify common audience interests; build novel occupation, relationships, setting, evidence chain, hard choice, timing and moral outcome. Do not reuse sample thumbnail characters or rewrite title story word-for-word.

### V1.1 newsroom test
For every PART identify one unique non-documentary emotional payoff and a different decision point. A document can catalyze conflict but cannot substitute for relationship escalation. Use a final pass to catch `modas de moda`-style repetition and witness entrance contradictions before handing to independent QA.
