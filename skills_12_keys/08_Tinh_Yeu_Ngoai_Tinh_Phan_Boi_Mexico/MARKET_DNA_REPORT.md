# MARKET DNA — MEXICO: AMOR SECRETOS Y TRAICIÓN (analysis of supplied dataset)

## Dataset scope
215 video records ordered by views, 215 attached example thumbnails, one representative MP4 (~6,212s). Records are a snapshot in the supplied archive; no upload-age denominator or impressions/CTR/retention analytics. Treat observed views as descriptive, NOT proof of causal virality. Captions directory has no subtitle files, so detailed transcript quality cannot be certified.

## Descriptive stats
- Video count 215; 96 have >=1,000 views, best 16,000, median 872, mean ~1,877 views.
- Runtime: 2,078–9,868 seconds, median 6,274 seconds (~104.6 min), average ~6,077 seconds (~101.3 min); top 25 median ~100.1 min.
- 3 duplicated title strings in supplied CSV; 53 of 215 titles use English lead-ins by simple pattern-based detection, despite Spanish content descriptions. Produce new final titles consistently in es-MX.
- Channel "tags" listed generic uploader words, not valuable research evidence; derive new metadata from real video content.

## Data-grounded strongest click structures
1. Public betrayal + status reversal. Top1 16k: husband brings mistress in heroine's dress, host publicly identifies heroine as creative director.
2. Immediate confrontation with compelling choice. Top2 12k: wine thrown at spouse, protagonist remains composed; violent escalation in sample thumbnail/title should not become default.
3. Abandonment at childbirth / pregnancy + absence/exposure. Top3 12k and top5 10k emphasize impossible emotional choice under vulnerable circumstances.
4. Priority given to former lover / childhood friend, lost relationship trust and irreversible exit. Top6 10k, top8 9.4k.
5. Husband/fiancé says fake/divorce plan, she signs and later he recognizes consequences. Top13 ~7.2k.
6. Corporate/financial or family leverage proved by records instead of magical instant possession. Top12 7.3k, top18 5.9k.

## Thumbnail evidence (visual inspection 001–035 of 215 provided)
- Dominant 16:9 text-heavy story images, left-aligned 45–55% yellow/white/red Spanish headlines on dark blocks; remaining area shows heroine's visibly shocked or composed face, dressed-up couple, hospital, gala, apartment, airport.
- High contrast warm cinematic composites, female foreground, antagonist in midground, occasional two-stage/collage visuals; sometimes bright turquoise or green highlights.
- Trailer reference MP4 is 640×360, long-form audio with persistent static artwork/social prompts and overlaid subtitles, not continuous live-action telenovela; create standalone natural narration + image-first packaging.

## What not to infer
We do NOT know exact CTR, watch-time, traffic sources, demographics, conversion, or why a given video gained views. Do not claim those metrics. Do not imitate a specific distinctive text composition line for line. A user's original creative differentiation matters more than mechanical title cloning.

## Narrative DNA families
- F1 `GALA_STATUS_REVEAL`: public humiliating event, unexpected earned public recognition, evidence chain, protagonist's professional identity.
- F2 `BIRTH_MEDICAL_ABANDONMENT`: rupture at childbirth / surgery, moral priority revealed, practical help and careful medical realism, boundaries and rebuilding.
- F3 `FIRST_LOVE_SECOND_CHOICE`: partner sacrifices heroine for ex/first love; heroine chooses dignity and real change.
- F4 `FINANCE_CONTROL_PROOF`: embezzlement/fake divorce/assets, traceable documents/ownership, plausible process and witness, no miracle court orders.
- F5 `FAMILY_BETRAYAL_ESCAPE`: in-law humiliation, withheld support, children/custody, informed exit, consequences.
- F6 `SILENT_EXIT_NEW_CHAPTER`: promises broken, quiet detachment, career/family growth, earned second romance optional.

## Strong story formula (not every story identical)
Promise in first 30–90s -> public/private betrayal -> tangible evidence -> proactive boundary -> believable new leverage -> opposition counters -> personal cost -> truthful reveal -> autonomous moral choice -> earned material/emotional consequence. Each Part grants a new earned answer and raises a meaningful question; don't rely on repetitive humiliation.
