# THUMBNAIL VISUAL DNA — 215 ATTACHED IMAGES; DIRECT REVIEW 001–035

## Grounded visual pattern
- Aspect ratio wide 16:9, heroine (25–50 if script appropriate) often near foreground/large face, formal evening wear at gala or hospital clothes in birth scenes, husband/mistress interacting in same cinematic setting.
- High contrast: black shadow background plus bold *yellow*, *white*, *red* text; occasional bright cyan and lime highlights. Warm cinematic lights and expressive faces, skin and fabric detail.
- Image text often 40+ words in reference, but better mobile readability may favor ~8–20 words, in max 2–4 bold lines. Keep subject and text distinct, safe margins 6–8%.
- Key tension compositions: heroine looks at couple; spouse turns away; woman holds proof; heroine leaving in foreground with antagonist behind. One visual story moment must be instantly parseable.

## A/B/C must test distinct curiosity anchors
A: `TRAICIÓN VISIBLE` — one scene, dark backdrop, wronged heroine prominent right foreground, cheating partner/ex behind, left copy with shocking action.
B: `PRUEBA LEGIBLE` — heroine with contractual/phone/object clue, supporting shocked face, 2nd color highlight on clue; no fake documents or legible sensitive IDs.
C: `DIGNIDAD Y CONSECUENCIA` — heroine calmly walking away or being acknowledged at a believable event, spouse reacts, viewer senses reversal but final ending hidden.

## Prompt template
"YouTube 16:9 1920x1080 cinematic Mexican telenovela still, *original fictional characters* consistent with script [specific ages/clothes/details]. SINGLE SCENE [exact location and event]. Foreground [heroine face and action], background [betraying person and witness if present]. Emotion [shock/sadness/controlled dignity], warm cinematic light, sharp human faces, clean left text zone 45%, dark negative space, photographic realism, no logos, no montage, no speech bubble, no watermark. Overlay exact es-MX headline: '[8–20 factual words]'. Colors yellow/white/red. Avoid text crossing faces."

## Production constraints
- Keep three prompts separately, independent files. Never ask a generator to copy exact 215 reference images or faces.
- Proper Spanish punctuation, accents and clear words; render typography separately in editor if needed.
- No excessively sexualized mistress/body, injury spectacle, violent retaliation, misleading pregnant medical suffering; keep attractive but respectful.
- If script is wedding/cafe, do NOT depict hospital; if script has no child, no child in thumbnail. QA factual alignment is paramount.
