# CLICK PROMISE & HONEST REVEAL DESIGN — V1.1 (STEP1)

## A title is an enforceable story contract, not a generic teaser
For EACH of the five title candidates, state:
1. `INSULT / LOSS`: exact relationship and concrete humiliating or threatening event that *must happen*.
2. `AUDIENCE QUESTION`: one unresolved question the viewer can phrase in 10 words.
3. `VISIBLE TRIGGER`: who touches, reads, sends or signs which object; do not promise magic online access.
4. `JUSTICE ACTION`: the father's own legally/practically possible choice; verb more precise than 'shocked' or 'regretted'.
5. `SOCIAL/EMOTIONAL CONSEQUENCE`: who loses power, changes a decision, pays a cost, or admits a truth; must be observable.
6. `PAYOFF SHAPE`: what a viewer will **hear/see** rather than only an attorney summarizing a document.
7. `EVIDENCE & ORIGINALITY`: what must be planted by Part 2, how it differs from competitor episodes.

## Calibrate title curiosity to script execution
- A title with 'he hadn't read the lease' promises **a mistaken assumption + the moment the assumption collapses + a tangible consequence**, not just repeated landlord/tenant definitions. The owner of the building may be identified early; the leverage, the son's realization and the personal cost must still escalate later.
- A title with 'they mocked my old job' promises the job skill creates a consequential action; don't solve it with unrelated wealth.
- A title with 'I found the file' needs legitimate provenance and visible discovery/verification.
- A title with 'my daughter...' is about her agency and relationship; never turn the loved one into an inert prize.

## Anti-anticlimax filter (pre-approval)
Reject or rework candidate BEFORE user lock when any applies: the promised result is entirely explained by the first 10% and no later consequence changes; the climax is an offscreen email with no earned human reaction; a surprise can only work if law or technology is implausible; a title implies malicious intent not supported by the outline; humiliation and its consequence are described but never dramatized.

## Evidence limits
Archived competitor views are snapshots, not audience-retention, CTR or proof a formula causes success. Internal 100-point rubric is a planning comparison, not a public success prediction. Keep current <=100 Unicode-character constraint and user title-lock discipline.

## Add to Step1 output, after 5 candidates
`PROMISE_TABLE`: 5 rows, one per candidate, with EVENT_TO_SHOW / WHO_SEES_IT / FULL_PAYOFF_EVENT / RISK_OF_EARLY_SPOILER / ORIGINALITY_DIFFERENCE. This is *analytical data*; do not write scenes in Step1.
