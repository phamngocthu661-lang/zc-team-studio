# Niche DNA: American Father Revenge — 8 practical story families

| Family | High-performing archive anchor | Audience promise | Mandatory novelty change |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: LANGUAGE / LISTENED QUIETLY | Secretly learned Italian, dinner (282k) | He heard the insult everyone thought was private | Use different competence/medium or genuinely distinct consequences |
| B: STATUS UNDERVALUED | Economy ticket/ID scan (95k), CEO/toilet insult (69k) | Status and dignity reversal in public | Status must be lawful and previously planted; agency stronger than logo on screen |
| C: WEDDING / HUMILIATION | Retired nobody at wedding (33k), bride-to-be expels father (22k) | Family reveals true loyalties during symbolic celebration | Avoid identical toast/CEO/guest-list tricks |
| D: FAMILY ASSET BETRAYAL | Daughter inheritance accusation (18k), fired from own store (16k) | Father learns to stop underwriting disrespect | Track ownership, trust, bank/account authorization and real timetable |
| E: SECRET WARNING / PROTECTIVE DAD | Daughter upside-down glass signal (18k), florist warning (19k), silent son speaks (17k) | Old father reads the one clue others ignore | Credible threat, victim has agency, evidence chain, no superhero detective |
| F: SKILLED RETIREE / PROFESSIONAL PAST | Retired NCIS investigator (28k) | Calm expertise beats younger bully | Choose modest tools/permissions; competence costs time |
| G: GRIEF / HIDDEN RECORD | Funeral→storage unit (12k), dead spouse records | Emotional trust reversal with tactile proof | No supernatural proof or instant probate shortcuts |
| H: DIGNITY & CONSEQUENCE | Daughter says step aside/stop calling (10k+), businessman collapse | Father chooses a boundary even if it hurts him | Consequence must be personal and reversible only through authentic change |

Rotating families: no more than 2 consecutive videos use CEO/hidden millionaire, wedding, trust fund, or 'I paid for everything'. Keep family relationship specificity, concrete U.S. setting, misjudgment, moral choice, and aftershock. Mix patterns for fresh premise, do not recombine competitor stories scene-for-scene.

Title patterns (formula, not titles to reuse):
- `My [relative] [humiliated me] at [event]—Then [specific credible trigger]`
- `I [quiet action] for [time]... At [event], [reaction action]`
- `My [relative] Thought [assumption]—Until [a document/person/object] [specific change]`
- `The [professional] Whispered [warning] Before [family event]—I [action]`
- `I Refused [demand] at [symbolic moment]—And [consequence only I could control]`

Keep the critical answer out of the title if a less explicit tease honestly describes the event. Do not promise scanner/authority scenes that script cannot technically deliver.

Every family must provide a concrete ACTION the father can choose and an OBSERVABLE human/family consequence. Prestige, paperwork and humiliation alone are insufficient as the full climax. See `CLICK_PROMISE_V1_1.md`.
