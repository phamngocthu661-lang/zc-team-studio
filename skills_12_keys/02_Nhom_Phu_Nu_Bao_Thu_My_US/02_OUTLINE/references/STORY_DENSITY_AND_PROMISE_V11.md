# STORY DENSITY / PROMISE / LEGAL PREFLIGHT — V1.1

**Retention and emotional credibility are designed in STEP2, not fixed by a surprise extra STEP5.** Long-form legacy format remains 7 Parts and 70,000–80,000 body chars; this quality gate prevents extending a thin 50-minute premise through repetitive explanations. If it cannot support the fixed format, return `REPLAN_BEFORE_WRITING`, strengthen the legitimate plot, or require explicit user approval to create a *separately versioned* shorter format. Never silently alter current tool length rules.

## Required 10 contracts in outline
1. `PROMISE_PAYOFF`: quote exact promise, identify the named payoff owner, the irreplaceable fact/record that owner provides, setup in early Part, final observable payoff Part and last line consequence.
2. `JURISDICTION_LOCK`: story's state, county/court or financial regulator context, service branch/status, controlling order date, source/relevance and proof gaps. No generic nationwide claims on family or property law.
3. `RELIEF_CAUSATION`: for each demanded remedy, identify initiating request, legally relevant facts, applicable standard or researched questions, opportunity to respond, hearing/deadline, court's findings. Proving one lie alone does not entitle heroine to a different custody schedule or automatic property award.
4. `EVIDENCE_OWNERSHIP`: each exhibit: creator, date created, custodian, recipient, access authority, integrity/authentication, fact supported, admissibility risks, and what it cannot prove.
5. `AGENCY_CHAIN`: every Part includes her specific choice -> reasonable immediate consequence -> unexpected cost -> changed leverage. She must cause or enable the reversal; rescue by a boss/commander/lawyer alone fails.
6. `SCENE_DELTA`: at least three scene state changes per Part. Each has TRIGGER, ACTION, DIFFERENT BEFORE/AFTER, NEW INFORMATION, COST, NEXT DECISION. No two scenes may have functionally identical informational and emotional changes.
7. `PROGRESSIVE_ESCALATION`: at least two serious escalations beyond initial accusation and one setback arising from heroine's own decision; at least two reversals must alter action options rather than just emotions.
8. `OPEN_LOOP_BUDGET`: at most three major loops alive at once; each is answered or deepened within a Part, and all title-critical loops are closed by end.
9. `PACING_SUSTAINABILITY`: target 70–80k body chars must be justified by unique dilemmas, distinct evidence and procedural time rather than repeated meetings and recaps. An outline is NOT fit if >20% proposed scenes merely recount previous facts.
10. `NO-MAGIC-REALISM`: check service member custody/procedural protections, school FERPA rights, shared/individual bank access, timelines for hearings, due process, marital property. Unknown state-specific law -> explicit RESEARCH_REQUIRED and STOP before drafting decisive scenes.

## Distribution targets and editorial signal
Target 74–78k if density allows; approximately 10–12 scenes per 10k chars, varied length. Each Part has at least one distinct critical action and a new fact; every 400–700 narration words a causal shift, but do not manufacture a cliffhanger every interval. More length does not equal more suspense.

### V1.1 JSON companion (new projects)
Alongside `CURRENT_FINAL_OUTLINE.txt`, prepare `STORY_PREFLIGHT_V11.json` following `references/STORY_PREFLIGHT_TEMPLATE_V11.json`, then run `scripts/story_preflight_v11.py STORY_PREFLIGHT_V11.json`. Tool checks completeness and structural causality claims only; creative and legal plausibility still require human/source review. If tool unavailable, provide the same fields manually and label `UNVERIFIED_PREFLIGHT` — never claim a machine PASS.

`STORY_PREFLIGHT_PASS` means structured planning is complete, not that the plot is legally correct or will perform better than competition. No change to hashes or existing executable script interface: the canonical outline remains `CURRENT_FINAL_OUTLINE.txt`.
