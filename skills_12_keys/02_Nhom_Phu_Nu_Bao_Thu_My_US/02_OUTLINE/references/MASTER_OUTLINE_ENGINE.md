# MASTER 7-PART OUTLINE / RETENTION ENGINE

Part 1 (~12%): Cold betrayal scene 0–5 min; no childhood biography; first protective decision; first verified anomaly.
Part 2 (~14%): Cost of silence, imminent risk, practical obstacle and villain motive; first action with real jurisdiction.
Part 3 (~15%): Investigation by own competence, independent verification and first public/financial win; reveal #1.
Part 4 (~15%): Midpoint reversal: apparent proof turns against heroine / ally conflicted; price of her strategic mistake.
Part 5 (~16%): Hard setback: antagonist counters within realistic power, threatening custody, career, access or reputation; isolate false solution.
Part 6 (~15%): Chain-of-evidence payoff, witness/document authentication, actionable legal/financial step with timeline; reveal #2/3.
Part 7 (~13%): Consequence with costs, earned boundaries and concrete emotional choice; final 1–2 lines reframe opener. No universal forgiveness, no fantasy instant convictions.

## Retention cadence
- Every ~400–700 words: a new decision, contradiction, proof, consequence or relational turn, NOT arbitrary cliffhanger spam.
- Each Part must include 3 scene state changes, at least one answer to an existing loop, and one forward question; do not add extraneous villains.
- First 5 minutes: (0:00–0:30) shocking actionable line; (0:30–2:00) stakes and credible relationship; (2:00–5:00) first clue and her choice.
- Clue introduced before payoff; evidence includes source, chain, access rights, authenticity, cost to obtain, witness proof and date.
- Morality: the woman chooses dignity and safety, not uncontrolled cruelty; consequences happen via earned documented steps.

## Mandatory 7-Part handoff table columns
`PART | scene | objective | trigger | time/place | evidence custody | heroine knows | opponent knows | authority/legal status | asset state | open loop IN/OUT | consequence | estimated chars`.
