# PRIMARY DNA `IRON_ROSE` — WOMAN SERVICE MEMBER / MOTHER / JUSTICE

Viewer fantasy: underestimated military woman can protect her child/parent, prove truth, hold an abusive ex or family accountable, without surrendering her humanity.

## Use one leading engine
1. **Unexpected return from duty** discovers child neglect, suspicious access, forged family story; her first action is to secure child safely and preserve evidence.
2. **Humiliation at family reunion/wedding** where her achievements/real duties are dismissed; evidence / witness exposes lie, not a deus-ex-rank handwave.
3. **Threatened custody/property during deployment** involving military family care plan, guardianship, residency and service obligations; court/legal action follows realistic process.
4. **Voicemail/footage exposed** leads from first-person account to actual chain of reporting police/command/legal counsel, not vigilante retaliation.
5. **Protective elder / veteran partner** against manipulation, housing or inheritance; earned allies intervene at realistic jurisdiction.

## Distinctiveness/novelty
Try roles other than always colonel’s daughter: Army Staff Sergeant, logistics officer, National Guard medic, Navy veteran, military spouse who becomes paralegal; avoid stereotype rank confusion. Show competence via decisions, paperwork, logistics, composure under pressure, emotional consequences and clear price of duty; authority alone never solves plot.

## Absolute logic guards
- Commander cannot arbitrarily order a civilian arrest or seize a private bank account. MPs and CID have limits; police investigate civilian crimes. Military justice and civilian jurisdiction depend on status and location.
- Deployment dates, leave authorization, child care guardianship, service branch ranks, uniform placement and emergency travel should be plausible and internally consistent.
- Nonconsensual or custodial acts involving minors are not gratuitously described; consequences prioritize child protection, safety, and evidence.
- Medical access and HIPAA/privacy, custody order, court service, police response are not instant; establish timeline, access, documentation and legal representation.
- Core emotional arc: betrayed daughter/mother/wife → strategic protector → principled agency → truthful accountability → family trust rebuilt or boundary secured.
