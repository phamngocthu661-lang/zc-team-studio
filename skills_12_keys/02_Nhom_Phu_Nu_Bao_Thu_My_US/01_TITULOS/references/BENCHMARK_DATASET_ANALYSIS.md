## Distilled repeatable but non-plagiarized formula
- Show a specific betrayal in the first ~0–150 seconds (not generic prologue).
- Make withheld fact identifiable by physical proof (voicemail, signed deed, timestamps, bank statements, receipts, medical records, photos, chain of custody).
- Protagonist chooses an intelligent irreversible move backed by her authority/control, not a deus-ex-machina rescuer.
- Reveal in ladder: first contradiction → document/identity → public/legal consequence → emotional redefinition.
- Retention open loops should cash out, not infinite fabricated cliffhangers.
- Villains retain realistic motives and means; no caricatured speeches; dignity/protecting child/boundaries beat sadism.
- Typical thumbnails sampled: dense first-person excerpt on high-contrast dark background at LEFT, right-side emotionally readable female portrait, yellow/red/green highlights; Quiet Triumphs occasionally uses hospital/kitchen/paper scene. Make visuals inspired by information hierarchy, not exact copied face or typography; mobile readability is essential.
