- First-person woman's POV and clear voice; military rank, combat deployment, custody, banking access, divorce, inheritance, wills, real estate and court procedure must be internally plausible, no instant magic government intervention.
- Require protagonist agency and specific credible evidence, escalation, irreversible decisions, satisfying consequences; no cheap fake twists or repetitive billionaire inheritance/phone-call rescues.
