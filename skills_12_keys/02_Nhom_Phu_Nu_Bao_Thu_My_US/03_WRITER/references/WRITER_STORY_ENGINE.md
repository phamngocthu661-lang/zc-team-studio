# NARRATIVE WRITER ENGINE / US WOMEN REVENGE

Narrator: always an adult woman, first person with constrained knowledge; retrospective framing permitted but every timestamp/date is stable. Opening hook: direct human injury/betrayal and a concrete detail that later matters. Voice is adult, calm, occasionally furious; not constant inspirational catchphrase. Blend dialogue, action, finances, emotions with controlled reveals.

Grounded cinematic details: cold floor of ER, unreturned phone notification, embossed deed stamp, unit deployment dates, security camera file timestamp, a child's backpack at the door, local courthouse hallway; each detail must affect plot. Avoid generic fog, purple metaphors, grandiose soap speeches, implausible overnight legal victories.

- IRON_ROSE: soldier's competence shown through duty, preparation, protective actions and follow-through, not a commander as private SWAT. Civilian safety via emergency services, reporting and court as appropriate; military processes described conservatively.
- QUIET_TRIUMPH: heroine's agency demonstrated through documented ownership, joint vs individual accounts, transparent asset inventory, legal advice and realistic timeline. Money is never conjured from mysterious inheritance without foreshadowing.

Before saving any Part: evaluate `WHO KNOWS`, `WHO CAN ACCESS`, `ASSET STATUS`, `LOCATIONS`, `DATE/TIME`, `PHYSICAL EVIDENCE`, `CUSTODY/SERVICE`, `PROMISE PAYOFF`. Never change canon without a traced cause. Every antagonist turn should answer heroine choice rather than magically predict her secrets.

Style: human American English; varied sentence length; no repeated 'little did he know', 'what happened next will shock', 'I smiled coldly' every 1000 words. No giant flashbacks before inciting incident. Never insert title/SEO/instructions inside story manuscript.
