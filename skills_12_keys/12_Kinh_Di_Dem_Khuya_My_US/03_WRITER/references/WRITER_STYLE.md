# NARRATIVE QUALITY BLUEPRINT — V1.1 US MIDNIGHT HORROR

For each American English 1st person story: tangible ordinary human objective; an initially dismissible but specific wrong detail; direct observations distinguishable from fears; two attempts at safe rational checks; rising practical cost; midpoint reversal that changes meaning of earlier evidence; a decision with consequence; distinctive aftermath.

## Effective audio horror
- Emotion through action under stress (hesitation at a door, difficult 911 call, a witness failing to see what narrator sees), not 500-word explanatory monologues.
- Sounds carry space: radiator tick, highway tire hiss, ice grinding against car well, a second locking click. Use them only when physically possible and connected to a clue.
- Vary cadence: brief sentences at moments of choice, longer precise observations when establishing evidence; first-person narration may be emotionally imperfect but cannot be omniscient.
- Reveal 2–3 clues gradually. Endings can be solved, mostly solved or intentionally ambiguous, but the ambiguity must be fair given observations.
- Distinct 8 narrators: vary occupations, home life, emotional stakes, slang restraint, age and education without caricature; **Default maximum three of eight stories have police/deputy/trooper confirmation as the MAIN closing payoff.** Characters may still sensibly call for help. Do not repeat six stories where everyone calls a deputy and receives an incident number.

## Anti-padding / anti-template QA before seal
Avoid repeated opening formula, `little did I know`, `I froze`, `my blood ran cold`, `nothing would ever be the same`, constant “I wasn't scared of X, I was scared of Y”, repeated `to this day I still...`, repeated 911 resolution, repeated person behind a vehicle, miraculous signal failure or functional camera in every plot. Check per-story repetitions and final two paragraphs against neighbors. No real victim names or claims fiction is verified crime reporting. Never write a how-to guide for coercion, violence or evasion.

Length: target N*~9k chars, sum consistent with budget; minimum substantive 5k each, default 8-story total 60k–83k hard and 65k–78k preferred. Estimated TTS runtime uses counted body words and explicit WPM assumption, not character extrapolation. If source uncertain, label uncertainty rather than invent evidence.
