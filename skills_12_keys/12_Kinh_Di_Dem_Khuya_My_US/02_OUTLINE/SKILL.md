# STEP 2 — OUTLINE & CANON (V1.1)
Read shared contract, `references/HORROR_DNA.md`, `references/OUTLINE_TEMPLATE.md`, and actual persisted `TITLE_LOCK.txt`. Never change the title without an explicit new project. No full narrative prose in this step.

## Story planning
- Build N separate first-person original American horror accounts (N=8 default), not one split serial. Use at least 4 DNA A–F for N=8. Story order places a striking but understandable immediate-hook in #1, avoids mid-video pacing troughs in #4–6, ends with highest conceptual residue in #N.
- Per story LOCK: original working title, region (state and plausible facility), month/year/time window, narrator age/voice and reason to be there, companion(s) and what each knows, map of paths/doors/sightlines, working phones/coverage, realistic nearby assistance, timeline of evidence, 7 causally linked beats, five escalating perceptions, rational alternative, first hard contradiction, irreversible decision, credible climax and aftermath. Every suspicious clue has an origin; don't reveal behind-the-scenes facts narrator cannot learn.
- Establish 2–3 **specific evidence checkpoints** from before midpoint that earn the ending. Record an evidence chain `observed → interpreted → independently corroborated or unresolved`; distinguish inference from fact. Establish realistic motive/constraint; no magical evidence and no implausible rescue within seconds.
- Diversify by narrator age/role, core threat (person, environment, technology, ambiguous), antagonist tactics, physical setting, evidence item, threat escalation and outcome. Reject if >=3 stories share the same "fake worker/stalker/unknown person outside" resolution or the same mandatory 911 climax unless each mechanism is substantially different. Police appearance is a possible outcome, not default ending. **Across 8 stories, no more than three should use official police/deputy/trooper confirmation as their main resolution**, unless the themed collection specifically requires law-enforcement cases; other stories may still responsibly seek help but resolve through different narrative payoffs.
- Plan per-story body codepoint budget; for 8 target 65k–78k body. Each 7,200–10,500 soft. Ensure sum of budgets equals planned target. Preserve narratable dialogue rather than artificial filler.

## Preflight / required files
`OUTLINE.md` containing all N seven-beat outlines, character and time/space constraints, `CANON.json` machine-readable keyed stories 1..N, `DISTINCTNESS_MATRIX.md`, `STORY_BEATS.md`, `TITLE_LOCK.txt`, `IDENTITY.json`. Save OUTLINE first, then calculate SHA from exact saved bytes and `video_id` per root contract; don't hash a draft outline and then modify it. The identity cannot be generated accurately until the saved title and outline both exist.

Run a scene-level preflight: narrators' knowledge, distances, US cultural realism, rights/provenance, timeline, clue causality and editorial differentiation. Block Step3 if missing meaningful story differences or broken geography; don't pad the count with plot clones.
