# FAMILY DNA LOCK V1.2 — NOT MORALITY-SHOCK / POVERTY-RESCUE CONTENT

**Source:** user editorial assessment of trial round 2, against Doña Elena / Sombras data in `SOURCE_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`, `COMPETITOR_HOOK_BENCHMARK.md`. This is a niche editorial policy, not an empirical CTR model.

## MANDATORY CENTER OF GRAVITY
- Default lead: Mexican **older mother or grandmother**, normally 60–80; she owns the narration, emotional stakes and **final meaningful choice**. Use first-person singular `Mi hijo...`, `Mis hijos...`, `Mi nuera...`, `Me...` by default. Don't replace her with a millionaire man, a child protagonist, stranger-beneficiary or a victim rescued by an unknown VIP.
- **The relationship itself is the wound.** Betrayer directly identifiable as child, son-/daughter-in-law, spouse, sibling, or consuegra tied to a concrete child/spouse complicity. A random snob, teacher, employer or passerby is NOT sufficient. A cruel in-law plus a passive, innocent son is weaker than son's own choice to betray or remain complicit.
- One **precise family violation** visible in the headline: refused birthday, house annexed, exclusion from wedding, abandoned at terminal, unauthorized sale, money taken, coerced signature. Ground moral outrage in an action someone can picture, preferably against a specific Mexican family expectation.
- One **visible consequence-bearing reversal**: refused signature requested by adult children, cancellation of voluntary guarantee, a boundary enforced by an actual owner, a physical choice with a cost. Evidence discovery or a phone call alone is not the reversal. The ending may withhold ultimate resolution, not what action or consequence the audience is promised.
- Reversal lever must be **hers**: her informed decision, legal rights where supported, history of work, written permission, consent or credible evidence; she is not made powerful by a surprise judge, billionaire savior, unrelated wealthy stranger or instant inheritance jackpot. Other relatives may react or help, but cannot replace her arc.

## HARD REJECT / REWRITE
1. Male millionaire pretending to be homeless to test his heirs = **OTHER NICHE**, reject even if viral-looking.
2. Janitor/poor worker mocked by strangers, helped by an unrelated authority = **OTHER NICHE** unless the actual perpetrator is a close family member and elder chooses the outcome.
3. Child with broken shoes as POV protagonist = **OTHER NICHE** unless child is contextual to an older mother's first-person family struggle and **not** the central protagonist.
4. Vague endings (`TODOS LLORARON`, `DESCUBRIÓ LA VERDAD`, `RECIBIERON UNA LLAMADA`, `NADIE LO PODÍA CREER`) cannot serve as the main reversal. Show a named demand, signature, refusal, departure, accountability or material/relational consequence IN THE TITLE.
5. `Me echaron de la boda de mi hijo` can be shortlisted but **culprit is grammatically unspecified**. To qualify as winner, establish who in the family chose to exclude her (e.g. son decided/assented) or do not claim strongest family betrayal.
6. A mother making a moral choice is not a revenge superwoman. The consequences are credible, proportional and emotionally costly. No eviction or bank-freeze fantasy.
7. A clickable title still fails if its promise cannot be developed as an original seven-Part, 70k–80k-character first-person family story.

## SEPARATE GATES — NEVER BLEND
`NICHE_FIT` (hard: protagonist, POV, culprit, family betrayal, heroine agency, concrete reversal) -> `CLICK_HOOK_STRENGTH` (priority) -> `STORY_FEASIBILITY` (floor) -> competitor benchmark (3 recorded anchors; no invented metrics) -> editorial winner. Strong shock cannot compensate for wrong niche. Human editorial sign-off must inspect the words, not just metadata flags.

## QUALITY-OF-HEADLINE MINI TEST
Can a woman 60+ in Mexico tell WHO among her family betrayed her, exactly WHAT they did, WHAT SHE can realistically decide, and WHAT the offending relative needs from her now? If not, revise. Do not copy competitor distinctive wording, exact events and reveal as a package.

## DIVERSITY WITHOUT DRIFT
Across five options, draw from >=4 of: unequal love / birthday, house/room takeover, family financial dependence, wedding/celebration exclusion, inheritance/signature pressure, physical abandonment, grandchildren used as leverage, family-chat betrayal, cruel daughter-in-law/mother-in-law preference. Settings and grievances may vary; **lead, family betrayal and dignity-through-agency never vary**. Do not make all five about a notary, deed or unexpected signature.

## VIDEO LENGTH / TITLE HONESTY
Production default remains EXACT `70,000–80,000 Unicode codepoints of body`, seven Parts, A/B/C. DO NOT say '30–45 minutes' as equivalent; runtime varies with TTS speed. To change duration, require explicit user selection and new cross-skill contract; no local unilateral Writer setting.

## TWO USER-FEEDBACK ANCHORS, NOT GUARANTEED WINNERS
- `Me echaron de la boda de mi hijo por vender tamales… y él dejó el altar para buscarme` is emotionally clear but does **not** name which family member expelled her or guarantee her agency; a **runner-up concept to refine**, not an automatic Winner.
- `Mis hijos me dejaron en la terminal de Puebla… 7 días después me suplicaron una firma` is closer to core DNA. It requires an already-established, lawful reason for requesting her signature; build that causal chain before approving any outline. Do not just insert a magic notary/suddenly valuable lot. The mother must still choose whether to sign.
