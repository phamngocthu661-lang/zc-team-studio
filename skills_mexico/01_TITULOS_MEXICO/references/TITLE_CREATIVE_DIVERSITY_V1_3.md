# TITLE V1.3 — FAMILY DRAMA HOOK VARIETY + UNPREDICTABLE REVERSAL

This reference is MANDATORY after the V1.2 FAMILY_DNA_LOCK and V1.1 competitor benchmark. Preserve all prior title and niche hard gates; the new gate does not replace them.

## Problem shown by 5 pilot titles
Four ideas effectively ended with `le negué / rechacé / le exigí devolver / rechacé` (withdraw money, deny occupancy, demand return of property, stop a sale). They differ in object but offer the same ending grammar. The mother has agency, yet viewers already know the response. A bigger insult alone cannot solve a predictable ending. A first-person older Mexican mother and an identified close-family wrongdoer remain NON-NEGOTIABLE.

## Four independent tests for every title
1. **Family wound:** what exactly did a daughter/son/nuera/yerno/consuegra do, and why is this specific to their relationship?
2. **Human contradiction:** what older assumption of love, duty, past sacrifice or unequal treatment breaks in this moment? Prefer observable contrast (mother vs consuegra; own room vs guest).
3. **Active collision:** who must now face whom, when and over what specific choice? Titles need not always expose the heroine's final answer; withholding a decision at a meaningful confrontation can be stronger than announcing `le negué`.
4. **Curiosity without fog:** one concrete obstacle or reversal signal + an *unresolved consequential question*. A vague `una llamada`, `todos lloraron`, `algo inesperado` is not a promise.

## DIVERSITY AMONG FIVE (portfolio gate)
- Before selecting, create a 5-row `DIVERSITY_LEDGER`: `family_relation; original affront; setting; tangible stake; resolution_family; hook_frame; audience_question; onstage_7part_payoff`.
- Require >=3 distinct **resolution families** among 5, <=2 using any one family; >=3 distinct **hook frames**. At least 2 materially different FAMILY CONTRAST situations, e.g., elderly mother compared with preferred in-law; one relative uses another as an alibi; hidden plan made in the mother's own property.
- Resolution families: `withheld_support` (refuse loan/gift/vehicle), `public_confrontation` (on-stage accountability), `reclaimed_space` (real enforceable possession/boundary), `reversed_dependency` (abuser must ask elder for meaningful help), `evidence_ambush` (proof changes a live choice), `family_ultimatum` (loyalty choice at real cost), `procedural_boundary` (signature/notice/consent with lawful authority), `unresolved_request` (audience awaits decisive answer), `other` (explain genuinely new physical action).
- A mere change of asset (`money` to `car`) does **not** create a new resolution family.
- Hook frames: `direct_quote`, `family_contrast`, `caught_in_the_act`, `humiliation_to_collision`, `unexpected_request`, `betrayal_plus_cost`, `other`. No frame by itself guarantees a good title.
- Titles may end in `me pidieron...`, `llegué cuando...`, `escuché...` IF the situation is concrete, has impending consequence and the 7-Part payoff is shown. Avoid forcing a premature revenge spoiler just to satisfy an action word.
- Across 5 options at most TWO may end with `le negué`, `rechacé`, `no firmé`, `le exigí`, `se lo quité` or syntactically equivalent refusal/withdrawal as the entire revealed ending. Mark not just literal synonyms but semantic equivalents.

## 3-PASS TITLE SURGERY
A) Generate ≥12 directions. Include at least 4 family settings and 3 different relationship contrasts. Eliminate male-millionaire, poor-worker rescue and generic moral fables.
B) Draft five promising **different scene mechanisms**. Compare head-to-head with real supplied E01/E02/S01 and mark DATA_LIMITED where same-DNA references absent. It is forbidden to claim outperforming on views.
C) For each: remove abstract revenge; rework a predictable final denial into a live confrontation OR a genuinely unexpected *earned* consequence. Do not add legal miracles. Test title aloud as a Mexican Spanish mother, count real Unicode length <=100, check correct kinship.

## WINNER SELECTION (editorial, NOT a CTR forecast)
Must pass V1.1 score gate, V1.2 niche gate, V1.3 diversity gate **AND human review**. Among eligible titles, favor emotional contradiction + specificity + still-unanswered family question over 1–2 points of model self-score. An individual title can be strong even if the five-title portfolio is repetitive; the set still needs a rewrite before declaring campaign-ready. Keep locked title immutable. If portfolio fails after 3 rounds, report NONE and actionable diagnostics.

## FIVE-TITLE PILOT: IMPORTANT CRITICISM
- `Mi hija me hizo servir en su boda ... y le negué ...`: vivid, but action predictable; a stronger pending family encounter may increase curiosity.
- `Mi hijo vació mi taller ... su suegra`: strong contrast of two mothers; future conflict must have a concrete paid cost, not just refusal of occupancy.
- `Mi hijo se burló de mis tamales ... le negué mis ahorros`: believable but both act and answer are revealed; explore unexpected dependency or what the business partners witness.
- `Mi hija me dejó sola ... le exigí devolver el coche`: emotional harm > vehicle consequence; consequence mismatch.
- `Mis hijos pusieron mi fonda en venta ... rechacé la oferta`: legal ownership must be verified; refusal alone is unsurprising.
These are editorial diagnostics, not official CTR measurements. Do NOT silently rewrite a user's already LOCKED_TITLE.

## Human reviewer "false-positive" prompts
Could the same story be told with five interchangeable insults? Does the title name the actual family wrongdoer? Is the mother deciding, or simply being rescued? Does it promise an event the script can legally show? Does it withhold a decision without becoming meaningless? Is it original vs competitor premise? An all-PASS JSON packet may still be editorially weak.
