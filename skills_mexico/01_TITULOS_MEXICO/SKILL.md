# SKILL 01 — TÍTULOS MÉXICO V1.2: FAMILY DNA + GANCHO + CONSECUENCIA

## MANDATORY SOURCE ORDER
Read `references/SHARED_CROSS_SKILL_CONTRACT_V1.md`, `references/MEXICO_FAMILY_STEP_CONTROLLER_V1.md`, `references/SOURCE_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`, `references/TITLE_DNA_MEXICO.md`, **`references/COMPETITOR_HOOK_BENCHMARK.md`**, **`references/TITLE_SCORECARD.md`**, `references/TITLE_OUTPUT_TEMPLATE.md`, and `references/TITLE_RED_TEAM.md`. This SKILL owns Step1 only; never outline, script, QA or SEO. Vietnamese production explanation; Spanish (Mexico) published titles. User-locked title is immutable.

## WHY V1 WAS NOT ENOUGH
V1 rewarded a believable clue, script feasibility and novelty so heavily that a title ending `encontré el anuncio` could outrank a title with **felt injustice + active reversal + unresolved consequence**. A clue alone is not an earned turning point. V1's 92–96 scores compressed distinctions: don't repeat this. The best-to-outline title is NOT necessarily the best candidate to test on YouTube.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE TITLE DNA
Each title has: (A) an immediately understandable *specific* affront by a named relationship; (B) visible human/material stakes; (C) a protagonist action, directly implied reversal or collision that changes what happens next; (D) a consequential unanswered question. For a non-violent family story, prefer earned reversal over empty outrage. A compelling contrast like *mi hijo prefirió cenar con mi consuegra* is stronger than the abstract phrase *me faltaron al respeto*. Use contextually plausible vocabulary and first-person voice (`mi hijo`, `mi nuera`, `mi yerno`, `mi consuegra`). Emotional charge must come from the moral violation, not a fabricated millionaire reveal or magically instantaneous eviction.

**BANNED WEAK ENDINGS unless followed by an actual consequence:** `les hice una pregunta`, `encontré un anuncio`, `guardé el mensaje`, `no dije nada`, `nadie podía creerlo`, `se quedaron helados`, `pero todo cambió`. They may be intermediary beats in a story; they may not be presented as the entire title's reward. `Un día lo sabrían` is likewise non-specific. A first-person counteraction need not spoil the ending; it must promise an irreversible choice or a collision.

## STEP1 WORKFLOW — REPEATABLE AND EDITORIAL
1. **Decode input:** `RELATIONSHIP / OFFENSE (exact action or quote) / PICTURE-ABLE OBJECT / LOSS / PROTAGONIST'S EARNED LEVER / COUNTERACTION / OPEN CONSEQUENCE / STORY LIMITS`. If no supplied premise, invent one plausible within Mexico DNA. Do NOT borrow competitor wording and distinctive twist as one package.
2. **Analyze competitor hooks:** using `COMPETITOR_HOOK_BENCHMARK.md`, for EACH of the final five candidates compare at least THREE sourced competitor anchors; preferentially three of same DNA when actually available; otherwise clearly mark closest same DNA and 2 adjacent. Never invent competitor titles or live view metrics. Write concise **why competitor sparks anger**, **which candidate beats or loses to it in clarity/action**, and **what question remains**. Snapshot views are context, not CTR evidence.
3. **Internal divergent generation:** create ≥12 rough ideas across at least FOUR primary DNA families, ≥3 kinship configurations, ≥3 distinct earned consequences. For each idea devise 2 title framings: `direct insult → reversal` and `evidence/contradiction → consequential next action`; do not dump internal brainstorming to user. Reject those copying source or depending on legal magic.
4. **Hook surgery before polishing:** title must show a specific injustice, not generic sadness; replace weak evidence-only endings with protagonist agency or a causally credible counterreaction. Contrast elder vs rival relative or elder's own home vs invader where story warrants. Keep under 100 Unicode codepoints, with natural es-MX and not all-caps spam. DO NOT promise an action Outline cannot fulfill.
5. **Two independent scores, no inflated blanket 90s:** assess CLICK_HOOK_STRENGTH /100 and STORY_FEASIBILITY /100 with explicit line-item deductions from `TITLE_SCORECARD.md`. Separate `ORIGINALITY_GUARD`, `LEGAL_MX_GUARD`, `DELIVERABILITY_GUARD` as hard gate PASS/FAIL. `EDITORIAL_COMPOSITE = round(0.75×CLICK + 0.25×STORY)` for sorting information ONLY; it must never override hook gate or head-to-head judgment. Scores are **not predicted CTR**.
6. **Red-team and benchmark:** ask the 5 tests in `TITLE_RED_TEAM.md`. Candidate must survive an honest head-to-head test: compared with top relevant examples, does it have equally legible wrongdoing, a consequential reversal signal, and a genuine curiosity gap? Record `STRONG / PARITY / WEAKER / DATA_LIMITED` with reasons, not made-up numeric competitor scores.
7. **Winner gate:** eligible only when `CLICK ≥85`, `STORY ≥80`, all hard guards PASS, both reversal and consequence visible, and benchmark verdict `STRONG` or `PARITY` against at least one closely matched source. Prefer strongest CLICK among eligible, then source-adjusted editorial head-to-head; higher STORY may resolve a close CLICK tie, not replace it. If all five weak, internally rewrite all five, up to 3 editorial rounds. If still below gate, output exactly five **honestly marked NOT READY** and `SUGGESTED_WINNER: NONE`, with a diagnosis; NEVER force a winner, fabricate >90 scores, or silently override a locked title.
8. **Handoff:** record exact locked title ONLY if user explicitly chooses; otherwise `PENDING_USER_SELECTION`. Preserve compact `TITLE_HOOK_BRIEF` including exact injustice, key contrast, promised protagonist action, what NOT to spoil, proof path, antagonist plausible motive, primary stakes, target climax collision, 7-Part feasibility, competitor comparison, and forbidden easy fixes. Step2 must honor this as a narrative contract.

## STRICT ORIGINALITY + MEXICO
Avoid lifting an exact competitor quote + structure + payoff, exact courtroom judge surprise, implausible ownership by paying predial, instant lockout, secret billionaire, automatic bank freeze, unauthorized recording, pressure using children as props, or real-story claims. Natural Mexico terms: narrator's son's wife = **nuera**, daughter's husband = **yerno**, mother of child's spouse = **consuegra**, narrator's own mother-in-law = **suegra**. If an exact bank/legal consequence is proposed, establish lawful authority and realistic time in the later outline. Family members are human, not cartoon villains.

## LENGTH AND VALIDATION
`≤100` Unicode codepoints including spaces, accents, punctuation; if Python runner exists use `len()` or `scripts/title_quality_gate.py`. The script validates packet structure, count and arithmetic, NOT semantic quality/CTR. Without actual calculation mark `UNVERIFIED`, never guess counts or benchmark metrics. Each of the five should differ in ≥2 of relationship/offense/setting/evidence/action/consequence and not be near-duplicate phrasing.

## OUTPUT
Exactly five visible titles, with separate CLICK, STORY, COMPOSITE, benchmark verdict, evidence of deductions, and length; head-to-head table and `SUGGESTED_WINNER` (or `NONE`) as specified in `TITLE_OUTPUT_TEMPLATE.md`. End with the controller's exact Step1 suggestion ONLY after user title lock; don't auto-run Step2. Do not print invented scores as empirical CTR statistics.
