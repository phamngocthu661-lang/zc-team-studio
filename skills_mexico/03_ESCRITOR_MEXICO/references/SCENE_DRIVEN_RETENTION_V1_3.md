Read after existing EMOTIONAL_RETENTION_ENGINE_V1_1 and MATRIARCH_FIRST_PERSON_STORY_V1_2. Preserve `OUTLINE.txt` SHA, writer A/B/C boundaries, exact 7-Part UTF-8 LF, codepoints and byte-safe repair procedure. Do NOT edit production Python scripts.

## A 75,000-character drama is not a license for filler
For each scene use `DESIRE → RESISTANCE → CHOICE → VISIBLE CONSEQUENCE → STATE DELTA`. Mark ONE dominant function and SECONDARY tension. If a paragraph merely restates "my children hurt me" or "I sacrificed everything", rewrite with an observable event, a specific dialogue change, or trim it. An emotional recollection may stay if it changes a live decision or gives a new interpretation.

## Scene variety budget (not a mechanical quota)
Within each Part alternate meaningful **pressure types**: money/house/occupation; attachment, shame, kinship loyalty; credible proof; procedural constraint; physical inconvenience; quiet memory with consequence; visible opposite party reacting. No three consecutive scenes composed solely of accusation + protagonist silence + repeated resentment. Never create arbitrary accidents/abductions or a mysterious benefactor merely to reach the character target.

## Seven-Part live execution
- P1 open in an exact title-related wrong by named family member. Elder does something tangible early, not only whispers a maxim. No reset into chapter-long biography.
- P2 expose contradictory family treatment through **real behavior**, not narration-only exposition.
- P3 impose first boundary; family counter-moves in a way that changes real options.
- P4 the viewer/elder learns a plausible new truth that changes her understanding or strategy; its clue MUST already exist.
- P5 force costly second strategy; the villain is not just shouting louder.
- P6 perform TITLE encounter physically, with the document/room/person/choice already prepared. The mother chooses; consequences touch both sides, not a speech-only punishment.
- P7 show the price and changed daily routine in an action. No sudden all-forgiven without accountability. No 2,000-char sermon.

## Mexican Spanish speaking voices
Narrator 60–80+: experienced, sometimes proud, contradictory, warm but not uniformly saintly. Each child has distinctive speech, excuse and memory; do not turn all antagonists into interchangeable screaming puppets. Stable `tú/usted`, `nuera/yerno/consuegra`. Oral clarity in es-MX; avoid subtitled-video transcription errors, awkward literal English idioms, endless `se me rompió el corazón` repetitions.

## EDIT-ON-READ before persisting A/B/C
At each Part end write a PRIVATE CHECK: 1 precise new offense/evidence, 1 elder decision with price, 1 antagonist adaptation, 1 paid micro-payoff, 1 question advanced and 1 state no longer reversible. Then scan 3000+ codepoint stretches with no new evidence/decision/consequence; **inspect and cut/redistribute them, do not blindly fail every reflective passage**. When characters repeat a conversation, require changed leverage and a different objective. Verify P4 actually changes hypothesis, P6 the promised on-stage payoff, P7 earned consequence. If NOT, mark EDITORIAL_NOT_READY even when technical character gate passes.

## STEP5 REPAIR INTEGRITY
If QA cites repetition or pacing, its Patch ID must identify exact byte window and desired scene change. Writer may alter ONLY authorized spans. Fix causality, not just word choice; do not quietly "rewrite all Parts" under a small pacing patch. Technical checksum and QA re-review are separate. Automated scripts cannot verify literary power or guarantee better retention than competitors.
