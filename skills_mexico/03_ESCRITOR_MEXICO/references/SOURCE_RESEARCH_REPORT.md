## Tested story DNA (descriptive, not mandatory for every title)
**DNA A — Cumpleaños / Exclusión:** emotional betrayal in milestone event; discovery of an alternate family celebration; ledger of invitations, expenses and phone calls. Promise: not invisible after being discarded.
**DNA B — Casa / Despojo:** nuera/consuegra assumes control of recámara or home; the protagonist understands documents/ownership and defends boundaries without instant eviction fantasy.
**DNA C — Dinero / Dependencia:** protagonist financed luxury, tuition, debt or family living expenses; decision to stop subsidizing; money and accounts must remain technically plausible.
**DNA D — Herencia / Firma / Notaría:** suspicious distribution of family property, manipulative relatives, actual paper trail and professional help; reveal must be fair and foreshadowed.
**DNA E — Mensaje / Boda / Nietos:** rejection/estrangement text, wedding seating or grandchildren access; preserve child agency, emotional cost and credible relationship repair if earned.
**DNA F — Testigo subestimado:** household worker, neighbor, receptionist, accountant sees a clue; must have agency, privacy and believable access; not a deus-ex-machina.

Old woman protagonist should have *specific earned competence*, e.g., ex-contadora de una cooperativa, pequeña empresaria, enfermera jubilada, maestra, comerciante de mercado, administradora de tienda; their choices operate inside real constraints. Distinct Mexican local environments (Veracruz, Puebla, Querétaro, Guadalajara, Oaxaca, Monterrey etc.) should appear only if functional and factually plausible.
