# WRITER V1.1 — EMOTIONAL RETENTION & CONFLICT EXECUTION

## Primary objective
A 75k-character script must feel **more alive and emotionally compelling**, not simply longer. Write character decisions that create new trouble. Deliver the exact Step1 and Step2 promises without copying competitors. No automatic guarantee of surpassing competitor metrics; stronger hooks need live CTR/retention testing later.

## OPENING GATE (first 5k codepoints, must be checked on actual script)
- First ~200: dramatic title event already taking place in observable dialogue/action.
- By 450: a concrete object/witness/message makes accusation falsifiable.
- By 750: the human contrast (my son chooses consuegra over me; my home becomes nuera's plan) touches the elder's deepest concern.
- By 1,300: narrator responds with a **specific** preliminary decision, not only 'me quedé callada'.
- By 2,300: antagonist responds or evidence contradicts easy explanation.
- By 5,000: second tangible proof + first consequence. Do NOT reset clock into a long flashback; embed memories into present action.

## PRESSURE / INVERSION BEAT
Each scene must dramatize `WANT → RESISTANCE → ACTION → COST → SHIFT`. At least two distinct pressure types per Part (material/relational/private shame/public comparison/uncertainty); do not replay same insults at higher volume. Major scene endings should change the question, not announce generic mystery. A 7-Part emotional plot should allow tenderness and loss between conflict beats. Dialogue takes effort: interruptions, repair attempts, details that the speaker would know, silences with consequences.

## CHARACTER-COST DUALITY
Protagonist has a legitimate claim AND a flaw: excessive sacrifice, fear of loneliness, pride in keeping family together, difficulty accepting adult child's choice. Every strong reversal forces her to give up a comforting belief or a practical benefit. Younger relative may need money or feel loyalty tension but cannot be excused from chosen harmful acts. One sympathetic relative should challenge the narrator with an uncomfortable fact; do not make entire family a chorus agreeing with elder.

## STRUCTURAL BIG MOMENTS
P1: offence on stage + act / P2: proof of chronic asymmetry / P3: first paid boundary + counterattack / P4: hypothesis collapse or unexpected cost / P5: personal pressure / P6: title's **promised action + consequence** shown in physical scene / P7: new practical life, no sermon. For title with `luego me suplicó que fuera su aval`, the demand and narrator's *actual* response need live dialogue, risk assessment and emotional cost; writing only `no acepté` in a paragraph is insufficient.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE ANTI-FILLER LINE EDIT
After every written scene, ask: if removed, which knowledge / financial balance / trust / relationship state / action changes? If NONE, shorten or delete and replace with a causally necessary scene. Reject repetitive reflective paragraphs (`mi corazón...`, `nadie imaginaba...`, `aquella tarde comprendí...`) when they do not add new information. Avoid a fresh cliffhanger at the expense of payoff; at least one meaningful question must close in every Part.

## SPAN CHECK BEFORE SAVE (narrative, not Python-only)
Mark `TITLE_PROMISE_DELIVERED` as a reviewer observation: exact offense occurs; decisive counteraction actually affects people in a lawful sphere; subsequent emotional loss shown; Part7 demonstrates what changed. Outline's promised action must be dramatized rather than reported. If any fail, revise script before technical verification, not by faking a perfect character count.
