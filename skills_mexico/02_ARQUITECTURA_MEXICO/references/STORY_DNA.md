# STORY DNA — 6 CLASSES FOR MEXICO FAMILY DRAMA

These are abstract source observations, NOT license to copy original reference scripts. Choose one PRIMARY + optional one SECONDARY. Justification should link to locked title's relationship/offense/action.

## A — LA MESA VACÍA (birthday/rejection)
Inciting event: family members deny time or attendance; protagonist sees celebration without her. Engine: contradictory invitation/billing/photo detail → one witness → discovery of social/financial dependency → chosen boundary. Emotional false belief: "si dejo de dar, dejarán de quererme". Core price: accepting an imperfect lonely birthday. Avoid clone of an existing restaurant encounter.

## B — LA CASA NO ES DE USTEDES (home control)
An in-law assigns a room or makes plans for a house without owner consent. Engine: unequal household expectations → original property arrangement → pressure using grandchildren → no that protects home → relational fallout. Proof: documents, keys, actual tenancy; not a magical instant eviction. Emotional object: table built by spouse, family recipe, old photograph, keys, access.

## C — LA CUENTA QUE NO VIERON (financing)
Child believes elder will fund lifestyle forever. Engine: bank access, invoice, responsible payment records, personal retirement security, boundary, financial consequence. Avoid investing jargon pulled from reference transcript and impossible asset freezing.

## D — LO QUE DECÍA EL TESTAMENTO (succession)
Dispute about an estate, hidden contribution or beneficiary. Engine: family misreading, notarial or legal procedural meeting, ignored smaller document, emotional cost of disclosure, lawfully earned resolution. NEVER equate being listed on a note with instant ownership.

## E — NO ERES BIENVENIDA (event exclusion / grandkids)
A wedding, posada, baptism, graduation or family group chat shames elder; child's ambivalence raises stakes. Engine: evidence of rejection + she refuses role as financial prop + hesitant bridge from one relative + accountability or dignified distance. Grandkids never simply become props for revenge.

## F — LA PERSONA QUE SÍ VIO (underestimated witness)
The overlooked neighbor, market assistant, caregiver, secretary, cleaner has a grounded observation. Engine: witness's risk + what could be observed + corroboration + adult protagonist's own action. Do not turn ordinary witness into convenient all-knowing savior.

## DNA pairing compatibility
A+C, B+E, D+B, E+C and F paired with exactly one of A–E are common workable combinations; limit escalation. A+E+F+hidden judge+poison is too much for believable family drama.
