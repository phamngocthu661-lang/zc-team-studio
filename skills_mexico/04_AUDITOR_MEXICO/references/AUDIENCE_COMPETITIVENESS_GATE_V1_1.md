# INDEPENDENT AUDIENCE / COMPETITIVE STORY QA V1.1

Read the full **persisted** Spanish script and exact locked title, Step1 `TITLE_HOOK_BRIEF` (if present), and Step2 `PROMISE_OWNERSHIP_MAP`. Apply independently of mechanical QA checkpoint. A high technical score, 168 filled cells and 75k characters are **NOT** evidence that viewers will enjoy the story or that title will outperform competitors.

## QUALITATIVE NON-NEGOTIABLES — if any fails, actionable QA defect and NOT READY
1. **3-SECOND INCITING OFFENSE:** literal title event manifests immediately; relationship, insult and stakes are apparent without 1,000 chars of exposition. Cite exact P1 span and character offset.
2. **COMPETITOR HOOK PARITY (EDITORIAL):** against E01/E02/S01 mechanisms, is wrongdoing as LEGIBLE and emotional contradiction as direct? Note nearest DNA and differences; source views cannot be treated as predicted CTR. If weaker, identify concrete script weakness; do not inflate 'PASS'.
3. **BEFORE/AFTER POWER:** what changes at P3, P4, P6? Demonstrate practical control / voluntary support / social accountability before vs after with exact scene evidence. Mere ownership discovery is not an action.
4. **TWO-SIDED FAMILY TENSION:** at least one credible nonvillain reason for conflict and protagonist's flawed response; no cartoon demonization, no sentimental saint.
5. **MID-STORY NO LOOPING:** P3–P5 must not be the same family argument repeated. Identify 2 genuinely new facts, 2 costs and 2 strategic changes; demand revisions if scenes are movable/removable.
6. **CONSEQUENTIAL CLIMAX:** promised title response happens as a scene, antagonist response affects future relationships, consequence follows real authority/legal rights. Not rhetorical question, found ad, magical judge, mass shock or sudden millionaire.
7. **COSTLY EMOTIONAL ENDING:** Part7 reenacts normal life with a changed action, unresolved grief and earned accountability. No 2k-character 'lesson for the audience' sermon.
8. **AUDIT AGAINST SCRIPT:** Cite *exact* Spanish passages or line/character anchors for each decision; empty checkmarks aren't evidence.

## QA FINDING FORMAT
`HOOK_DEFECT: H01` plus exact source P/anchor, `PROMISE CLAUSE`, evidence in script, `WHY THE AUDIENCE WOULD DISENGAGE`, causal root, measurable remedy, smallest repair-zone plan, rubric deductions mapped into EXISTING `qa_checkpoint.py` keys (often TITLE/HOOK, Retention/Pressure/Pacing, Agency, Mystery, Emotional Payoff). No extra rubric keys or structural changes to QA JSON. If structural deficit spans entire plot and cannot be patched without breaking locks, escalate upstream to Outline/title selection; do not authorize whole script rewrite as a tiny patch.

## CLOSEOUT
A separate manual `AUDIENCE_COMPETITIVENESS` verdict = PASS/NOT_READY/UNVERIFIED. READY requires PASS, in addition to ALL existing technical score/defect gates; never claim real-world superiority in views/CTR without published A/B and audience retention evidence.
