# INDEPENDENT V1.2 FAMILY-NICHE & STORY-QUALITY AUDIT

Cross-read title, outline, complete full script, existing audience gate and competitor anchor mechanisms. This review is **qualitative/editorial** and cannot be replaced by a high numeric QA score or by matching hashes.

## HARD DNA CHECK (P1–P7, with real exact Spanish excerpt references)
1. `OLDER_MOTHER_CENTER`: age/role/gender stable; key decisions, information access and ending belong to her, not a billionaire man/child/stranger.
2. `FIRST_PERSON_ES_MX`: single stable older woman's narrative voice, no accidental perspective switch or Spanish-ES drift.
3. `FAMILY_CULPRIT`: exact named son/daughter/in-law + meaningful complicity and recognizable motive. 'Strangers humiliating poor lady' alone FAIL.
4. `SPECIFIC_FAMILY_BETRAYAL`: event in first 200 chars, stakes and family history understandable, no vague distant moral puzzle.
5. `TITLE_TAIL_DELIVERED`: by P6 perform literal consequential act in title. `recibieron llamada`, `lloraron`, `encontró papeles` without a changing choice = FAIL.
6. `HEROINE_CAUSAL_AGENCY`: removal of her decisions would radically change outcome, no external savior/secret judge/honorific rescuer.
7. `CREDIBLE_STAKES_AND_PROCESS`: money, keys, ownership, signatures, notaría and Puebla time are supported where used; no magical power or phantom rights.
8. `PACING_NOVELTY`: P2–P5 each introduces new tactic, evidence, cost or changed relation; no long repeated shouting and apologies.
9. `EMOTIONAL_AFTERMATH`: P7 shows a changed concrete routine, mixed feelings and proportionate accountability; no instant reconciliation or long sermon.
10. `MEXICO_REALISM`: voice/relationships/cost of decisions realistic without conflating `suegra / consuegra / nuera`.

Verdict `FAMILY_DNA_QA: PASS / NOT_READY / UNVERIFIED`. For ANY failed check, record actionable Pxx on existing rubric key and block editorial READY; if missing source, UNVERIFIED. Record exact Part, searchable phrase and short cause/impact. Do not conceal issues in minor observations merely to score 98. QA checkpoint scripts enforce evidence/schema but cannot themselves certify this analysis.

## ADVERSARIAL EXAMPLES
- Millionaire disguised as homeless to test sons: FAIL protagonist and niche even if twist dramatic.
- Elder cleaning stranger's home and rescued by judge: FAIL family-centered conflict and heroine agency unless user scoped a different genre.
- Wedding exclusion where fiancé suddenly saves mother: title-tail may be emotionally memorable but heroine agency FAIL if no meaningful choice made by her.
- Adult children abandon mother in terminal and need signature days later: could PASS only if specific document existed before betrayal, motive/time are motivated, narrator has a lawful choice, and P6/P7 deliver it without court magic.

## RUNTIME/LENGTH
Only exact 70k–80k verified body characters is the production gate. If someone reports 30–45 minutes, request speech-rate evidence or flag `RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED`; do not quietly change character budget or falsely certify TTS duration.

## COMPETITOR EVALUATION
Benchmark emotional mechanism vs 3 sourced anchors (visible offense, family asymmetry, woman decision, genuine unresolved consequence) and state **PARITY POSSIBLE / WEAKER / DATA LIMITED** in editorial terms only. Measured 'better than competitor' cannot be verified from offline samples, and is not an audit pass requirement. The aim is stronger writing with originality and stable DNA, not fabricated superiority.
